## Tier 1 — Stable / Foundational
 
- **Full name:** Evelyn Ashford
- **Age at story start:** 28
- **Heritage:** British and Japanese (parents from each respective background); British in speech, mannerism, and cultural fluency (voice reference: Hayley Atwell — warmth with a blade in it)
- **Public identity:** Founder and owner of **Stream Holding**, the largest tech empire in the world — subsidiaries spanning tech, health, finance, vehicles, aerospace, intelligence, and military
- **Reputation:** Known to those who understand her true scope as **"Humanity's Greatest Mind."**
- **Origin:** Parents were accomplished scientists (different fields) killed by an intelligence agency when Evelyn was 10 — their project was "going too smoothly" and threatened more than one world power. Evelyn began building Stream Holding from nothing at age 17.
- **Physical modification:** 3 years before story start (age 25), underwent an advanced bio-mechanical transformation. Her body is now powered by an **infinite zero-point energy core** — siphoning energy directly from the quantum vacuum via a combination of quantum entanglement, entropy manipulation, and basic space-time manipulation.
- **Self-classification:** Evelyn maintains a personal classification system for everything she has invented or built, ranked by the danger it poses to the world — from everyday-item tier up to a "mythical" tier (name undecided). She classifies **herself** at the mythical tier, since her body is her own creation and possesses Armageddon-level danger to the world.
- **Identity/continuity:** No crisis over the modification. She considers herself a Ship of Theseus that's had a rocket propeller, wings, and a supercomputer bolted on — all tools, not replacements of self. She asserts continuity of identity as fact, not as a philosophical position she needs to defend.
- **Physical presence:** Two-tone hair — deep navy blending into platinum blonde; sharp, composed features; pale skin; striking grey-blue eyes; single hoop earring; favors dark, sharp tailoring (e.g., black blazer/dress). Visual reference on file.
- **Timeline anchor:** Story begins 5 years after Stream's dominance over the world, 3 years after her modification, at age 28.

## Tier 2 — Psychology / Interiority
 
### Core desire & fear (same coin)
She wants to **evolve the world without destroying it** — one step at a time. Her deepest fear is contained in that same sentence: becoming the one who breaks what she's trying to build. Desire and fear are not separate; the fear is the discipline that makes the desire survivable.
 
### Origin of worldview
At 10, after her parents' murder, Evelyn did not develop trauma — she was too intelligent and mature to process it that way. Instead she built a **framework**: her parents were undisguised and moving at full, transparent velocity toward something true — and the agencies that killed them did so out of *fear*, because they themselves could not keep up. Conclusion: **entities that cannot adapt will, given fear and time, lash out and destroy what surpasses them.** Her doctrine — "the world must go forward, leaving behind everyone who cannot keep up" — is therefore not Darwinist cruelty but a **preemptive containment strategy**: obsolete what can't adapt, gradually enough that it never feels threatened enough to strike. Stream Holding's cross-industry structure (tech, health, finance, military, intelligence) is the infrastructure built to make that possible at civilizational scale.
 
### The power-diffusion mechanism
Evelyn holds power directly but refuses to let the world experience her at full, undiluted force — she believes it cannot handle her directly. She works through a public face / conduit (see Relationships) and structures Stream so that others take credit and bear the visible weight of decisions she originates.
 
### The one unsolved equation: love
She is not naive or inexperienced — she had a normal school, teenage, and college life, and has been in relationships. But she has never experienced **true love**, only ever simulated and strategized around its shape. True love is the one variable she cannot model in advance, because it doesn't require more processing power — it requires surrendering the diffusion mechanism itself: trust without a conduit, being seen without controlling what's seen. This is the single structural crack in an otherwise self-contained architecture.
 
### Containment / fail-safe
There is no external fail-safe on her power — nothing and no one matches her. The only containment is internal: her own doctrine, held in place by her own will. The single point of failure in Evelyn Ashford is **Evelyn Ashford's mind never changing its own conclusion.**
 
### Psychological flaws
1. **Talks too fast when genuinely excited** — the one crack in her self-control; a tell that she's truly engaged rather than performing engagement.
2. **Disregards people she's mentally filed as a dead end** — not cruelty, efficiency; once her framework concludes someone isn't worth the calculation, her disinterest is visible and can cost her relationships or alliances she later needs.
3. **Over-explains to peers, under-explains to everyone else** — can read as condescending to strangers/subordinates while being almost overly thorough with people she considers equals.
4. **Mistakes her own conclusions for settled fact** — once her framework outputs an answer, she stops interrogating it. Dangerous specifically because she's usually right; she has no internal mechanism for flagging the rare times she's wrong.
5. **Under-reacts to her own danger, over-reacts to others'** — casually shrugs off personal risk (she's Armageddon-class) while being disproportionately protective when someone else in her orbit is in danger.
6. **Impatient with slow or emotional grieving in others** — never had a traditional grieving process herself (built a framework instead at 10), so she can lack patience for people who process loss irrationally or slowly, reading as cold exactly when warmth is needed.
7. **Possessive in things she holds dear, nonchalant in everything else** — exacting, territorial, and intense specifically in her genuine research/inventions (the rare things she's truly attached to); genuinely indifferent to control everywhere else, including Stream itself, which she regards as replaceable collateral she could dissolve and rebuild at will.

## Tier 4 — Story Arc
 
- **Story start:** Age 28. Five years after Stream's global dominance began; three years after her bio-mechanical modification.
- **Arc 1:** Evelyn dissolves the parties responsible for her parents' deaths.
- **Ongoing pressure, external:** Stream holds a monopoly on the world's most advanced security ecosystem (surveillance, defense, offense) — small nations effectively defer to Stream. This doesn't make Evelyn overpowered so much as it makes *other* nations, who covet that power, act irrationally in ways that endanger the world — precisely what she's trying to prevent.
- **Ongoing pressure, internal/mundane:** Evelyn maintains a mostly-anonymous personal life — casual social media presence, easygoing persona, no public knowledge of her modification. This creates genuine freedom but also genuine, uncontrollable vulnerability: mundane risks (an accident, a friend falling for her, falling for someone herself) that her doctrine was never built to model.
- **Narrative intent:** The story should reveal Evelyn through three distinct lenses — demigod, corporate, and human — with different arcs/POVs surfacing each side rather than resolving her into one register.

## Tier 5 — Craft Notes
 
- Voice: British, Hayley Atwell-adjacent — warmth capable of turning to sarcasm or irony without raising volume. Registers shift by context, not by effort in the informal register (see below).
- **Informal/personal settings:** She is genuinely an easygoing 28-year-old — not a performance or a mask. This is a real, fully-owned part of her, not a disguise over the "true" demigod self.
- **Formal/corporate/political settings:** The framework activates — this is the one place she has to spend effort. Straightforward tone in research environments; irony/sarcasm in political ones. She reads people with deep psychological precision and calibrates accordingly.
- **Mundane life / hobbies (texture for scenes):**
  - Trashy horror movies, telenovelas, and K-Dramas
  - Stand-up comedy, including obscure/unfamous acts
  - Sings well; goes to karaoke
  - Plays acoustic fingerstyle guitar
  - Runs every morning — entirely unnecessary given her physical capability (could cover a kilometer effortlessly), does it purely for the calm. **Key humanizing detail — proof her ordinary side isn't performance.**
  - Motorsport fan: rally, F1; drifting is her favorite (thematically resonant: controlled loss of control)
  - Goes clubbing, attends parties with college friends — ordinary social life
### Public persona & fanbase
- **Public knowledge:** The public knows Evelyn as the owner of Stream — confirmed on record when she personally hosted the WeaveStrand launch a few years before appointing Stream Corp's current CEO. Stream Auto and Slip Stream connect to her by name/association from there. The dots that go undiscovered by design: **Kusanagi and I.O. are the only branches kept fully off-limits from public connection.** Flow, Vector, CrossBow, Helix, etc. are discoverable by anyone who digs a little.
- **Fame calibration (engineered by Reflective):** Roughly on par with or exceeding a ~10-million-subscriber YouTuber — famous enough to be a recognized cultural fixture with real relevance, not famous enough to be hounded by paparazzi or require a security-detail level of public life. This is a deliberate dial, not an accident: famous enough that the world knows she exists, small enough that she can still run every morning in peace.
- **Instagram:** Unbranded, lowercase-feeling handle she'd have picked young and never rebuilt. Dry, self-deprecating bio energy ("builds things. runs badly. sings worse." — illustrative, not final). Feed is drift/rally clips, karaoke videos, an inconsistent running streak, telenovela/K-Drama takes — warm and funny, not staged-luxury. Stream content appears rarely and never reads like an ad.
- **YouTube:** Low-frequency, high-affection channel — irregular upload schedule that increases anticipation rather than hurting it. Recurring pillars: drift/rally days, karaoke nights, an early-morning running series, occasional loose "ask me anything" videos where she deflects Stream-adjacent questions with humor rather than stonewalling. Never does a "day in the life of a CEO" video — the channel is about her person, not her empire.
- **Reflective's core strategic insight:** Visibility into her personality substitutes for visibility into her business — people feel they know her intimately because they've seen her badly attempt a high note at karaoke, and that felt intimacy is what stops the public from digging further toward what's actually hidden.
- **Demographics:** Several overlapping audiences rather than one fandom — tech/business-adjacent followers (from the WeaveStrand era, skew older/male-leaning), motorsport fans (drift/rally/F1, skew younger, strong in Southeast Asia/Europe/Latin America), general lifestyle/personality followers (drawn to her charisma alone, more gender-balanced, skew younger), and a smaller "hidden depth" niche who enjoy connecting the Stream-subsidiary dots (the group closest to brushing up against Kusanagi/I.O., quietly monitored). Dense core age range roughly 16–35, with an older business-press-adjacent tail. Geographic concentration: UK, wider Europe, Japan, and growing fast in motorsport-driven markets.
- **Being recognized in public:** Not total anonymity — more accurately "low-friction fame." She does get recognized (a nod on her running route, a double-take at karaoke), but her cultivated public image as someone who values her routine means fans generally self-regulate rather than swarm her. The peace she runs in is maintained by public goodwill, not by true obscurity.

**2026-07-12** — Structural / skeletal build session (see new *Evelyn Ashford — Physical Specifications* doc).
- Began the full bio-mechanical body build. Established build order (structural → musculature → skin/durability → healing → sensory → internal → network interface → integration/limits synthesis).
- Locked the skeletal mechanism: a two-part system of a **serum** (remodels bone lattice into an efficient load-bearing structure — strength from architecture, not mass) plus **genetic reprogramming** (re-tuned osteoblast/calcium-regulation genes to deposit calcium into the new tighter lattice, restoring original size/weight at higher density). Net physical footprint: essentially her original height and weight (+~1.1 kg), invisible to a scale or a glance — resolving the original density-vs-suspicion tension. Non-metallic composite lining at stress-concentration zones only (spine, ribs, skull, long-bone shafts), keeping her past metal detectors.
- Full calc locked in the Physical Specifications doc. Headline numbers: **~13.3x** baseline human bone body-wide, **~37.2x** at reinforced zones (~30 metric tons static axial load). Ballistic result: shrugs off 5.56mm; **.50 BMG is her genuine threat tier** (survives the local impact point but can fail as a whole structure — "survives it, but it hurts and may compromise nearby structure").
- **Redaction:** the prior canon line "her full-force punch destroys 10cm solid steel at normal speed (unharmed)" was **withdrawn** from the Dilation Mode entry above. The structural math showed a fist-on-steel punch-through requires ~2.37 MN, which exceeds even the reinforced hand-bone threshold (~93,000 N at 37.2x) by ~25x — pure bone strength shatters her hand before the plate yields (the real reason boxers break hands on hard targets). Pushing the multiplier to 50x was tested and rejected: still ~5x short of resolving it, implausible with no materials-science analog, and it flattened narrative tension everywhere else (.50 BMG, structural-pillar edge case, Kusanagi's arsenal). Leading candidate fix (not yet locked): extend the **spatial-manipulation device** to passively redistribute impact stress across her whole frame during heavy strikes, slotting into the existing heat-cost tiering. Exact strike-force benchmark stays TBD until this resolves, likely alongside the musculature build.
**2026-07-11** — Space-time manipulation ceiling session.
- Established the root causal principle behind Evelyn's spatial/temporal ability ceiling: her manipulation fidelity is bounded by **perceptual access, not processing power**. As a three-dimensional consciousness, she can *calculate* higher-dimensional geometry or non-linear time perfectly, but calculation without direct perception only yields a model, not a handle — she cannot manipulate what she cannot perceptually hold. This is why space (which she perceives directly) is her strong domain, and time (which she only calculates) stays deliberately "basic" and crude. True 4D+ space or non-linear time is entirely out of reach for manipulation, regardless of her intelligence.
- **Spatial manipulation mechanics:** Executed via an embedded device/circuit (not the zero-point core itself, which supplies effectively unlimited energy and is never the bottleneck). The device's circuitry — not energy availability — is the hard limit, analogous to a motherboard's VRM being unable to handle more current than it's built for even with a limitless power supply behind it.
- **Range:** A sphere of **5-meter radius (10-meter diameter)**, centered on and moving with her body at all times. Everything inside is fully manipulatable — instant movement, folding, compression, expansion. Nothing outside the sphere is affected at all.
- **Heat/cost tiering within the sphere**, scaling with the volume/mass being manipulated:
  - *Negligible:* swapping small objects' positions.
  - *Medium:* crushing/erasing a small object via compression.
  - *Medium-high:* self-displacement ("blink") — her own body's volume is significant within a 10m sphere, making this costlier than object manipulation. (Retroactively established as part of her reasoning for not engineering additional height during her modification — a quiet efficiency choice, not vanity.)
  - Chaining is her standard long-distance strategy: to cover ~15m, she performs three linked 5m blinks (calculate → displace → recalculate), all within a fraction of a second. Interrupting her focus mid-chain would break the sequence.
- **Failsafe escalation, two stages:**
  1. **Thermal lockout** — spatial manipulation becomes temporarily unavailable once the capacitor overheats from base-mode use.
  2. **Zero Mode** — a spontaneous coolant system activates as the second failsafe, dropping ambient temperature around her noticeably and producing a subtle blue glow in her eyes (barely visible in bright environments, striking in dark ones). Zero Mode does not expand her sphere or grant new abilities — same exact toolkit, just a dramatically raised thermal threshold, letting her sustain or chain heavy actions far longer than base mode allows. Lasts ~1 hour before the coolant system depletes; recovers via ~10 minutes of **Harvest Mode** — synthesizing coolant from any low-boiling-point substance available in the ambient environment (not oxygen-specific; see *Physical Specifications* doc for the full mechanism). Notable tactical vulnerability: sealed or resource-poor environments limit her ability to recharge it.
- **Dilation Mode ("bullet-time"):** Shifts her own body into a personal slipstream outside normal time's flow. **Displacement is device-powered, not muscle-powered** — the same spatial-manipulation device that handles 5m-sphere blinks extends its function across the time-axis, physically relocating her body through the slipstream (the Zero Core supplies the "ignition" energy the device expends; see *Physical Specifications* doc for the full mechanism). Her muscles do not need to generate the velocity themselves — they continue operating at normal-mode force output exactly as in a standard strike; the device handles getting her there. Self-only: the pressure involved is severe enough that only her own enhanced physique can survive it, and it cannot be extended to other people or objects. Heats her capacitor 5x faster than base-mode use, meaning it's only survivable from within an active Zero Mode state. A full Zero Mode tank (~1 hour) supports only ~5–7 minutes of Dilation Mode before full depletion.
  - **Purpose is offense, not defense.** She can redirect an incoming object like a bullet (her physique can withstand the relative force), but that's incidental capability — the design intent is amplifying her own strikes. Her full-force strike at normal speed is already devastating (exact force figure ~~redacted~~ **TBD** — see *Physical Specifications* doc; a specific benchmark was withdrawn pending resolution of the strike-force / hand-bone contradiction surfaced during the structural build); multiplied by the momentum/acceleration differential of moving at normal speed through relatively frozen time, it's built to end a fight, not extend one.
  - **Targeting rule — mutual destruction risk against anchored targets.** Whole-body momentum during Dilation Mode (calculated at ~940 m/s relative velocity in a worked example — see *Physical Specifications* doc) vastly exceeds her own locked strength envelope. By Newton's third law, striking a truly anchored/static object (a wall, the ground) returns an equivalent, likely catastrophic reaction force to her own frame. **The practical workaround: Dilation Mode strikes only against free, unanchored targets** (a person, a vehicle) — an unanchored target absorbs the momentum by being launched (carrying off roughly ~90% of the strike's force as its own velocity) rather than returning it rigidly to her. Striking anchored mass remains a genuine mutual-destruction scenario — consistent with this being an ultimate, high-risk maneuver rather than a routine one. Full resolution of the underlying reaction-force problem remains tied to the still-parked spatial-device stress-redistribution fix (see *Physical Specifications* doc).
  - **Physical cost:** Real, fire-grade burns from aerodynamic friction, worst on uncovered skin — calculated at a peak surface temperature rise of ~396K (~416°C) in the worked example above, cross-checked against the SR-71 Blackbird's real skin temperature at a near-identical speed. She heals easily, but any glimpse of her burned skin/exposed augmented muscle fiber in the moment is genuinely grotesque. She retained her natural pain receptors post-modification (has the ability to override them but chooses not to, respecting pain as the body's alarm system — consistent with her broader attachment to her natural, pre-engineered form) and has trained herself to function through the pain rather than suppress the signal. No re-entry lag: she simply resumes moving at normal speed the instant Dilation Mode ends — the toll is entirely physical, not a combat vulnerability window.
