# Evelyn Ashford — Residence
 
*Living document. Last updated: 2026-07-10*
 
---
 
## Overview
 
Evelyn maintains multiple properties across different countries, but this is her **true home** — the one place she actually lives, as opposed to the others which serve as functional footholds tied to Stream's global footprint.
 
- **Location:** New Jersey side of the Hudson Palisades, cliffside, facing the Hudson River toward the Manhattan skyline.
- **Distance to city:** Maximum 30 minutes' drive to the city border at normal speed.
- **Household presence:** No live-in staff. AEGIS manages the property; one or two manufacturing-purpose androids (same lineage as Kusanagi's automated workforce) handle anything physical that needs doing. Evelyn's privacy at home is absolute by design — nobody but her has ever seen the space unfiltered.
- **Guiding design brief:** Modern, not mansion-like. Able to conceal an underground lab larger than the house complex itself. Positioned to overlook the surrounding area, befitting the owner of a tech empire — while remaining inconspicuous enough that a presidential-level defense system underneath goes completely unnoticed.

## Exterior & Architecture
 
- **Form:** A cantilever-cluster hybrid — several minimal glass pavilions connected in two different ways, rather than one continuous structure or a single dramatic cantilever. At least one pavilion cantilevers directly out over the cliff edge.
- **Materials:** Concrete, glass, and steel on the exterior — cold, precise, unreadable from a distance, consistent with everything else she builds. Warm wood dominates the interiors, a deliberate contrast: public floor, hidden warmth, the same doctrine expressed on a different axis. Likely opportunity to thread in Japanese-influenced material choices and joinery (engawa-style transitions) without ever stating it outright.
- **From across the river:** Reads as a quiet, scattered composition of light and glass — a clean architectural statement, nothing more. No visible indication of what's underneath.
### The Main Cluster
Connected by an open-air walkway. Glass-forward with minimal privacy buffering, since the view-facing glass has no possible sightline in from outside — this is the core of Evelyn's actual comfort zone, where she is fully at ease (including, per Agung's note, walking around unselfconsciously after a shower or swim).
 
- **Living/social pavilion** — where she'd bring the rare guest.
- **Private/personal pavilion** — bedroom, personal retreat, hobby space (guitar, karaoke-adjacent).
- **Cantilevered study/office pavilion** — her real workspace, and the concealed entry point down into the lab (see below).
### Detached Pavilions
Connected via regular outdoor pathways rather than the open-air walkway — functionally separate from the main cluster.
 
- **Guest space**
- **Garage** — houses 6+ cars.
- **Gym**
### Gardens
Not a separate, defined space — integrated seamlessly throughout the entire compound, threading between and around every pavilion. Doubles as Evelyn's running track, especially during busier periods when a dedicated run elsewhere isn't practical. Reinforces that her morning run isn't performance — it's built into the literal architecture of her home.

## The Office
 
Function-forward. Not staged for anyone — no one enters this space besides Evelyn (or AEGIS). Nothing here needs to explain itself.
 
- **Workspace:** A big desk, documents scattered, holographic displays, tablets, prototypes — organized chaos that only makes sense inside her own head.
- **The bookshelf:** A real, working collection she returns to — her own published research, other scientists' published work, and philosophy. Not a prop.
- **The door:** A steel door concealed behind the bookshelf — deliberately styled as the classic "bookshelf door" trope, which Evelyn finds fitting rather than needing. AEGIS-gated: opens only for her, no manual mechanism, nothing to defeat because there's nothing physical there to find.
- **AEGIS's presence:** Materializes in the room as whatever persona it chooses (to be developed separately) — not a fixed terminal or screen.
---
 
## The Elevator & Access Logic
 
- Behind the steel door: an elevator with no visible floor buttons. Evelyn doesn't need to state her destination — **AEGIS reads which floor she wants from context** and takes her there. She can say it out loud if she chooses to, and occasionally does, but doesn't have to. (Flagged as a natural comedy beat: AEGIS guessing wrong.)
- **Authorization is tiered, not binary:**
  - Floors 1–2 (research, medical) are the only ones Evelyn could theoretically justify bringing someone else into, under extreme circumstances.
  - Floors 3 and 4 (command center, armory) are absolute Evelyn-only.
  - Floor 7 (AEGIS) has no "normal" access process at all — access is a deliberate, rare decision each time, by both of them.
- **No visible security theater** — no badge, no biometric scanner as an object. Authorization is ambient: AEGIS simply recognizes her.
- **Failsafe:** AEGIS is built on failsafe over failsafe; the only circumstance in which AEGIS becomes unavailable is a genuinely dire one — total compromise or physical destruction of its servers. In that scenario, the last failsafe is Evelyn herself: she manually breaks into the elevator shaft and accesses the floors by force. One of the only moments in her characterization where her Armageddon-tier classification becomes something *shown* rather than stated.

## The Lab — Seven Floors
 
Deeper into the cliff with each level; floor 7 is the deepest point of the entire compound. Floor heights vary modestly from level to level, though not dramatically.
 
| Floor | Function |
|---|---|
| **1** (uppermost) | **Basic research** — where Evelyn sketches rough ideas, produces crude prototypes via 3D printing. The one floor casual/low-stakes enough to (in extreme circumstances) bring someone else onto. |
| **2** | **Medical bay** — where her own bio-mechanical modification took place. Advanced, mostly robotic medical equipment; arguably capable of curing most diseases on Earth, or cloning a person. Also technically within the "could bring someone else" tier. |
| **3** | **Command center** — Evelyn's access point to I.O. Evelyn-only. |
| **4** | **Armory** — Evelyn-only. |
| **5** | **Mezzanine** — overlooks floor 6; doubles as a cataloging/inspection point for what's stored below, without needing to walk the floor itself. |
| **6** | **Storage** — houses "mythical"-tier items, mostly Evelyn's own creations: failed experiments, unreleased prototypes, and similar. Scaled for human/android-sized objects rather than vehicle-scale items. |
| **7** (deepest) | **AEGIS** — houses AEGIS's entire physical existence: rows of Carbon supercomputers, billions of petabytes of storage. Evelyn rarely visits this floor — and, notably, so does AEGIS itself (played for dry comedy: the equivalent of AEGIS never looking in a mirror). |
