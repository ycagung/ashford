# Trans-Dimensional Metaphysics — The God, Causality, and the Beacon Event
 
*Living document. Last updated: 2026-09-19*
 
---
 
## Overview
 
This document locks the metaphysical framework underlying the story's fantasy-genre expansion layer: the nature of the god, the structure of time and branching, the rules governing causality, and the mechanics of the beacon event that makes Earth accessible to other worlds. Everything here is built on a single standing principle carried over from Evelyn's own design philosophy: **perceptual access bounds manipulation fidelity, not processing power.** Every capability and every limit below — for the god, for branch-travel, for Folder tech — is derived from that same rule applied at different scales.
 
The core discipline governing this entire layer, per author direction: **every fantastical allowance gets a mundane, physics-honest receipt.** Nothing is permitted purely because it serves drama. If an in-universe capability isn't grounded in extrapolatable real physics or a clean logical extension of an already-locked rule, it doesn't go in.

## Tier 1 — The Nature of the God
 
- **Gender:** Deliberately, permanently ungendered. Referred to as "they."
- **Power level:** "Somewhat omnipotent," explicitly **not omniscient**. Reads as limitless from a 3D/4D perspective the way humans would appear limitless to a 2D being — but the limits are real, not merely hidden from the reader.
- **Character framing:** Not a moral agent. Acts as a **force of nature** — closer to a self-correcting balancing mechanism than a being with intentions toward individuals. Does not reward or punish; responds to conditions.
- **The late-story reveal (locked, not to surface until the final act):** the god is not the most powerful entity among fourth-dimensional (or higher) beings. A governing structure of some kind exists above/among their tier — shape not yet decided (candidates: mutual-restraint compact between transcendent peers; a hierarchy of dimensional scale where each tier can't fully perceive the tier above; or a plural/collective structure where "the others" are differentiated facets of one larger cognizant whole, not separate individuals). **Left open for a future session.**
### What "above time" actually means for them
 
- The god does not experience time as flow (tensed, "now becoming then") — that sensation is a symptom of being embedded *inside* the dimension, not a property of time itself. From outside, time isn't a river; it's a fully laid-out shape, the way the full length of a hallway exists regardless of whether anyone is walking it.
- The god does not "move through" time either — that phrasing still smuggles in sequence (step to step, point to point). Instead, they have **direct, non-sequential access** to any point — reaching in the way a 3D hand touches any point on a 2D plane without traveling across the intervening plane.
- Because the god still decides, weighs, and reacts (implying some form of before/after even for them), they are assumed to possess their **own axis of "becoming"** — a meta-time that is to them what ordinary time is to us: invisible as a concept, felt only as duration. This is the same stacked-dimension logic as the governing-body question (each tier's "up" is the next tier's ordinary ground), and it is what eventually allows them to be caught off guard by something on their own axis — a clean, non-arbitrary reason they are not the most powerful entity in the end.
### The cyclic structure — locked
 
The branch tree is not open-ended. The god perceives it as a closed loop — a torus ("donut"), not an infinite spray of diverging lines from a single point:
 
- **The origin point and the final resolved state are the same location**, not merely similar. Every branch, no matter how wildly it diverges, eventually reconverges — not into identical content (not a rerun of exact events, an eternal-recurrence loop), but into the same underlying **structure**, once scale and content are stripped away. This maps onto Penrose's real, published Conformal Cyclic Cosmology: a maximally-diluted, maximum-entropy heat-death state (the far side of the donut) and a maximally-dense, minimum-entropy origin state (the near side) are geometrically indistinguishable once nothing with rest mass remains to measure scale against — same shape, opposite cause of its own featurelessness.
- **This resolves the first-cause regress problem differently than the "no edge" framing above:** not an edge with nothing before it, but a loop where "before the origin" and "after the end" name the same coordinate. The origin is self-caused by the full converged weight of every branch feeding back into it — a third category, distinct from both "uncaused edge" and "infinite unresolved regress."
- **Life is structurally confined to one side of the loop.** The far side (post-infinite-divergence, maximum entropy) is a state where nothing with rest mass, chemistry, or structure remains — not hostile to life, but a state where "life" stops being a coherent category at all, the same way "up" is incoherent to a Flatlander. Evelyn's present moment sits on the near side, pre-infinity — the only side of the loop where life, and therefore any of this story, can occur at all.
- **This reframes the god's choice of "when" to intervene:** not merely picking the moment Evelyn's causal-density spike is ripe, but recognizing this as being within the one finite window in the entire cyclic structure where intervention is even meaningful — too early, nothing exists yet to correct; too late (far side), no one is left of the kind being balanced on behalf of.
- **Explicitly out of scope, not merely undecided:** whether a different kind of life could exist after reconvergence, in a subsequent cycle. The god does not concern themselves with this — it falls outside any timescale relevant to their current intervention, the same way a human doesn't factor heat death into a decision made today. Never intended to become a livable story location.
### The Flatland limit (why "somewhat omnipotent" is rule-bound, not just peer-bound)
 
- A being confined to 2D cannot conceive of "up" — not from lack of intelligence, but because their causal/perceptual framework has no axis for it. The god is assumed to have an equivalent blind spot: some further axis above whatever they operate in, which doesn't render in their framework at all. Not a choice to withhold power — a structural non-existence of the option, the same category as Evelyn's "beyond 3D + linear time is entirely beyond manipulation" limit.

## Tier 2 — Time, Causality, and Branches
 
### Causality, defined
 
Causality is the relationship where one event's occurrence is **the reason** a later event occurs — not mere sequence ("A then B") but production ("B, specifically because of A"). A branch of time is not a place; it is an unbroken **causal chain** — every event in it exists because something specific before it produced it.
 
### Scale of a branch — corrected
 
**A branch is an entire universe, not a planet.** One branch = one complete, fully-laid-out causal history: every galaxy, every planet, the full spatial extent, and the entire time axis from origin to (if any) end — all under **one fixed set of physical laws.** Earlier phrasing in this document referring to "Earth's branch" was imprecise and should be read as "Earth's universe" — Earth is one point of interest inside a structure that already contains every other planet, civilization, and era within that same universe.
 
- **Same tree, different branches:** same fundamental physical laws, but the causal history diverges at some fork (a decision, a quantum outcome, etc.) — two branches in one tree can both be "a universe with gravity/electromagnetism/quantum mechanics as we know them," just with different histories inside that shared rule-set.
- **Different tree entirely:** a genuinely different rule-set — not "history went differently" but "the laws themselves are structurally different."
This directly resolves how the four incoming-being categories can include beings "from other times" without violating the locked branch-crossing prohibition (see below): those arrivals are not literal time-travelers. They are beings from **other planets within the same branch/universe** — reachable lawfully via Folder — existing at the *same* cosmic moment but at wildly different points in their own civilizational development (a world still in an early era, or one far more advanced than Earth). "Other times" describes relative developmental era, not a different point on the time axis. True cross-branch or cross-tree beings have no lawful route in via Folder at all; their presence, if it ever occurs, would have to be an actual fragment of the beacon's own miracle-shockwave — the same uncaused-cause mechanism that empowers individuals — making them a mechanically distinct, exceptionally rare category, not merely a more exotic version of an ordinary Gate arrival.
 
### The block universe is a tree, not a line
 
- A "fully laid out, static shape" does not require a single strand. It only requires the whole structure — however many forks it contains — to be complete and unchanging as an object, the way a fully-grown tree has every branch and fork already existing simultaneously the moment you look at it.
- **Probability is what branching looks like from inside a single strand.** An embedded observer can't perceive the other branches, so an open future reads to them as uncertainty. That uncertainty is a symptom of limited perceptual access, not a real property of reality. From outside (the god's view), there is no "which way will it go" — every branch already exists, none privileged as "the real one." What reads as probability from inside is unresolved geometry from outside.
- The god sees the entire branching tree at once, all branches equally real and present — full structure, not a single fixed story and not a predictive fog. This is explicitly **not the same as omniscience**: seeing the whole tree's shape is not the same as knowing which branch a given mind will actually choose to walk. That gap is where the god can still misjudge something on the ground.
### Can a 3D+time being cross branches? — **No. Full stop.**
 
This was worked through as a multi-layer thought experiment and locked as a hard rule:
 
1. **The category problem:** A 3D+time being's causal structure only has axes for space and (embedded) time. Branches are separated along a further, orthogonal "modal" axis. Asking a 3D being to cross it is the same category error as asking a Flatlander to step "up" — the axis doesn't render in their causal structure at all. Not a strength/technology gap; a structural absence.
2. **The deeper problem — existence is a process, not a location:** A being is not a portable object riding on top of its causal chain (a rock that could be relocated). A being's continued existence **is** the causal chain continuing to locally produce it — closer to a flame than a rock. A flame isn't "in" the fire the way a rock is in a box; it is a sustained process that stops the instant the sustaining conditions stop. There is no substance called "the being" independent of the causal chain producing it, moment to moment.
3. **Consequence:** Being separated from one's own branch does not strand a being somewhere hostile — it removes the only condition under which the being was ever occurring in the first place. No destination is even relevant, because there was never a portable "it" to arrive anywhere.
4. **What real branch-travel would require, if it were ever possible:** not enduring existence outside a branch, but somehow being a **self-sufficient, portable causal engine** — independent of any branch's sustaining chain, so as to remain a genuine "output" even when unplugged from the source that was producing you. This is explicitly not "carrying your own causality with you" in any survivable sense (see below) — it is closer to arriving as an uncaused cause.
### Why "carry your own causality" fails as a workaround
 
- A being's causal history is not a short, containable string (parents → grandparents → etc.) that could plausibly be scaled up. It is the being's **entire past light cone** — the full causal history of the observable universe converging on that one point (stellar nucleosynthesis, galaxy formation, tracing back toward the Big Bang). This is not a scale problem solvable with more power or better engineering. It is structurally impossible for anything still embedded in causality to hold — a cup cannot contain the ocean it is floating in.
- **Open, deliberately unresolved question (matches the story's discipline of parking genuine physics ambiguity rather than forcing an answer):** whether there even *is* a single "cause of all" (e.g., the Big Bang) is left open. One honest out: causality is a relationship that only makes sense *within* time; if time has an edge, the chain isn't infinite, it's bounded, and "what caused the Big Bang" may be a category error (like asking what's north of the North Pole) rather than an unanswered question. This is left ambiguous even to the god.
### Miracles — the actual, precise definition being used
 
- Given the above, the only way to cross a branch, or to produce any effect with no antecedent in the local causal chain, is to **arrive as a genuine uncaused cause** — the rigorous, non-flavor-text definition of a **miracle**.
- **Clarified resolution to the apparent paradox** ("the uncaused event is still caused by something"): "uncaused" does not mean "causeless" in an absolute sense. It means **not produced by anything inside the causal chain in which it appears.** The event has a real origin (e.g., the god's choice, Evelyn's causal-density spike) — but that origin sits outside the jurisdiction where cause-and-effect is the local law. From inside spacetime, tracing only the internal chain of "this because of that," the event has no antecedent — not because it is lawless, but because its true cause is extrajudicial: a verdict from a court whose ruling was never on this side's docket.
- This is why a miracle is simultaneously explicable (to the god, to the reader) and genuinely irreducible from the inside (to anyone, e.g. Valley, trying to reverse-engineer it using only in-universe causal tools).
- **Locked, hard rule:** branch-crossing is categorically impossible for any being embedded in causality — including Evelyn. No future technology tier, no engineering workaround, ever closes this gap. Anything that appears to cross branches is, by definition, a miracle performed by something ontologically outside the chain (the god, and possibly their peers/superiors).

## Tier 3 — Folder (Gates): Lawful Physics, Not a Miracle
 
- **Explicitly not a form of branch-crossing.** Folder technology moves a being across *distance*, not across *branches* or *time*. It is named "Folder" in-universe (coined later by a character who identifies the mechanism) because it **folds space** — a real point-A-to-point-B geometric shortcut, not a modal-axis crossing.
- **Why this keeps it lawful rather than miraculous — proof sketch:**
  1. General Relativity permits stable wormhole-like geometries as legitimate mathematical solutions (Einstein-Rosen bridge, 1935) — not an invention for this story.
  2. The only known requirement to stabilize such a geometry (prevent instant collapse) is sustained **negative energy density**.
  3. Negative energy density is not hypothetical — it is experimentally confirmed via the **Casimir effect**: two uncharged conductive plates placed extremely close together in vacuum restrict which vacuum fluctuation wavelengths can exist in the gap, producing measurably less vacuum energy inside than outside. This has been measured in labs since the 1990s (the plates are pulled together by the resulting real, measurable force).
  4. Therefore, Folder tech is "just" an engineering problem — scaling negative energy density production by gargantuan orders of magnitude beyond lab-trace amounts — not a violation of physical law. Same category as a combustion engine: obeys every rule, the entire difficulty is raw capability, not permission.
- **Negative energy density, clarified:** not "less than zero" energy in an absolute sense — energy density measured *relative to the empty vacuum baseline*. The Casimir gap has fewer allowed quantum vacuum fluctuations than the surrounding vacuum, producing a real local dip *below* that baseline.
- **Contrast with tachyons (raised for comparison, then ruled out as the wrong analogy):** tachyons are permitted by the relativistic energy-momentum equation but have never been observed — pure unconfirmed theory. Negative energy density graduated from theoretical prediction (1948) to lab-confirmed phenomenon. Folder tech is grounded in the *confirmed* category, not the *tachyon* category.
- **Locked constraint to keep Folder lawful:** Folder must fold **space only** — same branch, same time-slice. A time-like fold (connecting two different *times*) would generically produce closed timelike curves under GR, reopening the causality-violation/miracle problem. Folder never moves anyone across *when*, only across *where*.
- **Narrative placement:** Folder tech sits in the same broad physics family as Evelyn's zero-point energy core (quantum vacuum manipulation, entropy manipulation, basic space-time manipulation) — whoever discovers it either builds on research adjacent to Evelyn's field or arrives at it independently, which would itself serve as a second real-world confirmation that this domain of physics is genuine within the story. **Open: which of these two paths is used — left for a future session.**
- **The god's actual role regarding Folder:** the god does not build or grant Folder technology. Folder is legitimate engineering that existed as a discoverable possibility already. The god's role is limited to whatever reveals, permits, or triggers its discovery (or it may simply be independently discovered as a downstream consequence of the beacon event's conditions) — reserving "uncaused miracle" status exclusively for the beacon event itself, not for the technology that follows from it.

## Tier 4 — The Beacon Event
 
### Why Earth, why now
 
- The god's function, as a force of nature, is to keep advancement across civilizations roughly balanced and predictable. Other civilizations advance on a natural, gradual timeline their own causal fabric can absorb.
- **Evelyn broke that pacing.** Her zero-point core concentrates causality into a small localized space beyond what her branch's fabric is built to absorb — a causal-density spike, structurally analogous to a mass-density spike collapsing into a black hole. The god does not react to "humans got too advanced" in general — they react to **concentration**, a specific, localized disequilibrium.
- Left unaddressed, this concentration would eventually **fold space violently, rip the fabric of time, and send a ripple through the branch structure** — a threat to more than just Earth's branch.
- The god, perceiving the full branch tree, identifies and chooses **the single ideal point in time to intervene.** This choice of *when* is the god's only deliberate, intentional act in this entire sequence.
### The nature of the intervention — one miracle, not many choices
 
- The beacon event is **one singular uncaused act** — the god striking the bell, not selecting which molecules move.
- This single uncaused event does not stay a single point of effect. It **propagates outward through the causal fabric like a shockwave**, and wherever it happens to pass through a person, an individually uncaused "miracle" of empowerment occurs at that point.
- **Critically, this is not selection.** No criteria, no chosen ones, no proximity requirement to a physical Gate. The scattering of empowerment across many individuals (rather than one counterweight) isn't a separate design choice — it is simply the statistical shape one indiscriminate, reactive shockwave produces at scale. Distributed empowerment is the consequence of the mechanism being reactive and unselective, not the mechanism itself.
- This also explains why hostile and monstrous beings arrive alongside empowered humans and friendly/intelligent visitors: they are not a judgment or a punishment layered onto the event. They are simply also close enough to the rupture — proximity to the wound, not selection by the wound.
- **Genuine, structural blind spot for the god (reinforces their established non-omniscience):** they can see the shape of time well enough to choose the ideal *moment*. They cannot necessarily resolve, in advance, the fine-grained result of releasing one uncaused event into an already fully-detailed causal fabric — precisely who it touches. They know the bell will ring. They do not know which molecules move first.
- **Open, flagged for a future session:** whether the empowerment shockwave has any texture at all (decay with distance from Evelyn, clustering by trait, or purely, irreducibly random) — currently left undecided.
### The god does not perceive Evelyn — or anyone — as a person
 
The god registers the anomaly Evelyn's Zero Core produces as a **structural condition in the causal fabric**, never as an individual. The same way gravity does not "know" there is a star at a given point — it responds to a mass concentration, and the star is simply what that concentration happens to be made of — the god perceives an untenable causal-density gradient, not "Evelyn Ashford, person, acting deliberately." This is consistent with, and reinforces, the locked non-omniscience limit: perceiving the *shape* and *severity* of an anomaly precisely enough to choose the ideal moment to act does not require perceiving the *subject* behind it. There is no version of events in which the god deliberately targets, spares, punishes, or rewards her specifically — there was never a "her" in the god's frame to begin with, only a gradient.
 
This extends to every miracle the beacon event produces, including any human interpretation of having "communicated" with the god (see empowerment shockwave, above): any such experience is manufactured entirely on the human end — raw, structureless causal contact landing on a mind that happens to organize it into something legible. The god contributes contact, never content, and never recognition of who received it.
 
### Evelyn's incapacitation — the mechanism, corrected
 
An earlier framing of Evelyn as the physical **epicenter** of an outward blast was considered and explicitly rejected: being the loudest point of a power-*granting* shockwave would make her its single largest recipient — the exact opposite of the intended outcome (evening out the concentration, and keeping her unempowered by this event).
 
**Locked mechanism: pressure equalization, not blast radius.** The beacon event is not an explosion centered on her that also happens to hurt her — it is the causal-density surplus she has been continuously producing (via the Zero Core's direct tap into the same substrate causality itself pools in) being **forcibly redistributed outward**, the way gas rushes from high pressure to low pressure the instant a seal breaks. She is not struck. She is **drawn from** — the source, not a recipient.
 
- **Physical vector:** the Zero Core is not a finite reservoir (it draws continuously from the quantum vacuum, "effectively infinite" per the Physical Specifications document) — there is no stored charge to empty. Instead, the equalization event acts directly on the Core's already-established distribution architecture: every receptor-gated discharge point across her entire Power Distribution Unit network fires simultaneously, forced past the existing >100% voluntary-override ceiling, system-wide, with no voluntary control involved. This uses existing, already-locked hardware (the addressable, graduated PSU architecture) rather than inventing a new mechanism — the "surplus" being equalized away is, mechanically, the Core's output, discharged through the exact routing her own body already has in place.
- **Result:** a full-body systemic overload event — not an injury with a clear wound, not an attack with a traceable vector. Closer to a total, involuntary emergency shutdown/deep-repair state than an ordinary injury. AEGIS can stabilize but cannot meaningfully diagnose a cause, since there is no causal chain to trace — functionally identical, from Evelyn's and AEGIS's side, to asking what caused the Big Bang.
- **Interpretation on the ground:** neither Evelyn nor AEGIS can establish any causality for the event despite being capable of establishing almost anything else. It reads, to both of them, as something close to a "divine wrath" they cannot disprove or explain — despite the god never having perceived Evelyn as a subject at all. The horror of the event, for her, is partly that it is real, catastrophic, and permanently inexplicable in her own terms.
**Detailed physical/neurological sequence, locked:**
 
1. **Pain, at full fidelity.** Because pain signals ride the same 4x nerve-conduction pathway as every other sensory system (already locked, Section 5 of the Physical Specifications document — no filtering mechanism exists), the total-network discharge registers as the phenomenological equivalent of a century's worth of pain compressed into a single instant.
2. **Neural system damage.** Not merely a signal overload — the discharge fries substantial real neural tissue, not just her subjective experience of it.
3. **Cardiac arrest.** The heart is one of the network's primary conversion nodes; the same overload driving every other discharge point stops it outright.
4. **Total systemic shutdown.** An extension of the same biological logic as her existing local governor (a Golgi tendon-inhibition reflex, not willpower) — scaled to the whole network, since a local governor was never built to arrest damage at this scale. Consciousness is not lost gently; it is forcibly cut as the last available protective response.
5. **Survival condition — the Zero Core is damaged, not destroyed.** The Core itself continues producing *some* output, but at a small fraction of prior capacity, and even that reduced output cannot be fully carried or directed, since the downstream Power Distribution Unit circuits and voltage regulators are themselves broken by the same event. She survives on whatever trickle can still reach viable tissue.
6. **Recovery mechanism — already-established spinal regeneration, at its true (non-Wolverine/Deadpool-tier) pace.** Her existing spinal-cord self-regeneration function, previously locked as honestly slow and non-miraculous, is the only repair mechanism at work. Five years is that function grinding, at its real established rate, toward the bare minimum threshold required to sustain consciousness again — not toward full restoration.
**Consequence — she does not wake up at full power.** The five-year mark represents the floor sufficient for consciousness, not the ceiling she had before the event. Core output, PDU integrity, and full prior capability remain diminished on waking, to a degree not yet specified — deliberately establishing that this is a partial return, with further recovery (or a permanently altered ceiling) to be built out separately. This directly changes the shape of the story going forward: not a narrative about an overpowered person encountering a new threat, but one about an overpowered person **rebuilding what was taken from her**, with her pre-event scale now a memory and a target rather than a given.
 
**Five years also remains the scale-calibration anchor.** The amount of surplus causality the Zero Core had been concentrating — sufficient to become the origin point for every empowered individual on Earth (see `Fantasy_World_Power_System.md`) — is measured by this recovery time. Useful later for illustrating exactly how disproportionate Evelyn's true scale is compared to anyone newly empowered by the event.
 
### The thematic core this locks in
 
Every enhanced person on Earth, regardless of Type or Tier, can trace their power back to the same single discharge. Evelyn will eventually be able to look at any of them — weakest to strongest — and know, factually, "your power came from mine." She is denied the ability to resent this: no empowered individual asked for what they received, and even where sanction might exist for a want, the transfer occurred by physics, not consent, on either end. She becomes, involuntarily, the first and largest casualty of her own doctrine — *"the world must go forward, leaving behind everyone who cannot keep up"* — applied to her at a scale she never accounted for, by a mechanism with no author to hold responsible.
 
### Sequencing note
 
Evelyn's incapacitation occurs **simultaneously** with the beacon event, not as a delayed consequence — it is not a separate reaction to the intervention, it *is* the intervention, viewed from her side. AEGIS experiences it as one unified catastrophe rather than a mystery that develops over time. Humanity's awareness of Evelyn's disappearance (the world noticing the disappearance of the person literally holding its tech infrastructure together) unfolds afterward, and separately, from the gates/Folders and the scattering of empowerment worldwide — three visible consequences of one invisible cause, none causally linked to each other from the outside.
 
### Zero Energy Conservation — the governing closed-system law
 
**This is the single hardest, most load-bearing physical law in this entire framework, and everything else in this section is a derived consequence of it, not an independent rule.**
 
> **Evelyn's Zero Core is the sole connector between Earth and the true zero-energy origin.** Without it, no new zero energy can enter Earth's atmosphere or causal fabric — before the beacon event, Earth held effectively none at all outside her own Core. The total cumulative zero energy present on Earth is therefore fixed for all time at whatever quantity the beacon discharge delivered, decreasing only by a minuscule, constant rate of natural degradation and leakage back to outer space. **How that fixed total is distributed or held — by individuals, by the Earth's crust, redistributed violently by a later human-caused event — can fluctuate freely. The total itself cannot grow.**
 
Every other principle below is this law, applied to a specific question:
 
- **Rate versus total.** Total energy released by the discharge = output rate × duration. The world's cumulative share (summed across every Enhanced individual, every gate, and the Earth itself, over the full duration of the discharge) is an *energy total* — comparable to Joules. Evelyn's own capability is a *power rate* — comparable to Watts, an instantaneous peak. These are different physical quantities, and a large total says nothing about who held the higher rate at any given instant. A small rate sustained long enough can still produce an enormous total.
- **Conservation of peak instantaneous rate.** Every Enhanced individual and every gate draws on a *fragment* of the one discharge event — none of them is an independent new source. A fragment of a whole can never exceed the *peak intensity* the whole reached at its most extreme instant. This is what makes it true, not merely asserted, that **no Enhanced individual, at any Tier, and no single gate, can ever exceed Evelyn's own peak output** — the aggregate can dwarf her total energy contribution to the world, while every individual piece remains permanently capped below her ceiling.
- **Growth is real but rate-capped.** Enhanced individuals who grow stronger by absorbing ambient leakage (from gate residue or from the Earth itself, see below) are still drawing from the same finite, already-released pool. Growth increases how much of the available fragment a person holds — it never raises the ceiling itself.
- **Redistribution events are possible; new-supply events are not.** Because the total is fixed and only its *distribution* can move, an extraordinarily large-scale human-caused event — violently seizing and relocating a huge share of already-existing zero energy into one place or one act — remains lawful under this system and can look, in its effects, genuinely apocalyptic. What such an event can never do is exceed Evelyn's own peak instantaneous rate, or add one unit of energy the beacon discharge did not already release. (See `Fantasy_World_Power_System.md`, the Metano section, for the single most significant real-world test of this exact boundary.)
- **Consequence for her own diminished state.** Evelyn's own output at the moment of discharge is what set the ceiling every fragment is measured against — and that same discharge is what depleted her Core to a small fraction of its prior capacity. Locked: **on waking, her output is low enough that she is functionally reducible to an ordinary, unmodified human baseline** — Zero Mode, Dilation Mode, and the network link effectively offline, not merely reduced. This is the concrete answer to the previously-open "degree of diminishment" question.
### Consequences of the beacon (established, cross-referenced with earlier project scope)
 
1. **Gates/Folders** — a separate, parallel, lawful-physics consequence of the same root trigger (Evelyn's causal-density spike). No causal link between Gates and who gets empowered — two independent effects of one cause. **Two independent variables govern gate distribution, and they must not be conflated:**
   - **Quantity — deterministic, not random.** Gate density is highest near the Arctic (the beacon's point of origin — see Tier 5) and attenuates with distance, consistent with a finite, physically real energy pool thinning out with distance from where it was released. A nation's total gate count is therefore a function of *both* its proximity to the Arctic *and* its sheer territorial area — a large nation gets more chances at a gate forming within its borders than a small one, independent of location; a nation close to the Arctic gets a higher density per unit area than one far away. These two factors compound (e.g., a large nation near the Arctic gets the most gates of all; a small nation far from the Arctic gets the fewest).
   - **Quality/destination — genuinely, irreducibly random, per individual gate.** All gates are equal in kind — the same physics, the same mechanism — but *where each one connects to* (which world, which era of civilizational development, hostile/friendly/monster-producing) is not determined by location, national wealth, or anything else. This is deliberately not weighted by geography at all. A gate-poor small nation can get exceptionally lucky with its one gate; a gate-dense Arctic nation can be unlucky enough that all of its gates are hostile or monster-producing. Quantity is geography; quality is a lottery, and the two never correlate.
   - This gives the Arctic and its bordering nations outsized strategic weight primarily through *quantity* — more rolls of the dice, not better odds per roll — a distinction worth preserving in how the political landscape (see `Post-Beacon_World_State.md`) discusses "why the Arctic matters."
2. **Less intelligent, more powerful beings arrive** — monsters.
3. **More intelligent, less hostile beings arrive.**
4. **Intelligent and hostile beings arrive** — the most dangerous category.
   - All incoming beings view Earth through some combination of: a new playground, a new power source, or (if both) a new place to conquer.
5. **The Earth itself absorbed a considerable share of the released energy — locked, geophysical consequence.** Not every fragment of the discharge became a gate or an Enhanced individual; conservation of energy requires the remainder to manifest somewhere, and the two most physically honest sinks for raw energy dumped into a planet are heat and mechanical stress.
   - **Two compounding factors, mirroring the gate-quantity model:** Arctic proximity sets the baseline intensity available in a region, while **existing geological weak points** (fault lines, dormant volcanic systems, thin crust) determine where it actually manifests — energy concentrates where the crust already offers the least resistance, the same way electricity finds the path of least resistance rather than spreading evenly.
