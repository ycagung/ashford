## Overview

This document locks the classification framework for empowerment resulting from the beacon event (see `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`). Five things are tracked here: **The Interface** (how a mind physically touches zero energy at all), **Type** (what kind of power it is), **Subtypes** (the internal breakdown within Somatic and Kinetic — Metano has none, see below), **Hybrids** (what happens when a person's conversion spans more than one Type), and **Tier** (how strong it is — currently frozen, see banner below).

**Standing constraint carried over from the metaphysics document:** the empowerment shockwave is genuinely uncaused, unselective, and without intent (see Tier 4 of the metaphysics doc). No classification system built by humans changes that underlying truth — Type, Subtype, Hybrid status, and Tier are all post-hoc human attempts to make sense of something that was never designed to make sense in the first place. Any apparent order in any of them is discovered, not built-in. **Vital is the clearest case of this yet** — see below.

**Every ability in this document, without exception, draws on the same fixed, non-renewable pool of zero energy** — see `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`'s **Zero Energy Conservation** law. Evelyn's Zero Core is Earth's only connection to new zero energy; the total on Earth was fixed the instant the beacon discharge ended and can only ever be redistributed afterward, never replenished. Nothing in this document — no Type, no Subtype, no Hybrid combination, no Anomalous case, however extreme — is exempt from this.

**Evelyn's relationship to this system is locked** (see Tier 4 of `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`): she is not empowered by, or classified within, this system at all. Every unit of energy in existence — every Type, every Subtype, every Hybrid stack, every Anomalous case — traces back to a single source: the total causal-density surplus discharged from her Zero Core during the beacon event, redistributed with zero intent and zero selection. She is the origin of the aggregate power supply this entire system measures, not a participant in it. Note: some pure Somatic Strength-types can exceed Eve's raw output outright (see Somatic below) — her ceiling comes from architecture, not from competing on this axis.

## The Interface — how the mind actually touches zero energy

**The problem this closes:** every Enhanced person can store zero energy in their body (a direct consequence of surviving assimilation) and passively recharge from ambient atmospheric zero energy (the same slow leakage the Zero Energy Conservation law already accounts for). Neither of those facts explains how a mind ever *directs* that energy. "They control it with their mind" is not an answer — no mind controls anything directly, including its own body. A mind only ever controls a body by firing neurons at tissue built to respond. Zero energy is no exception, and this document does not treat it as one.

**The real-world grounding: electric organs already do exactly this.** Electric eels, rays, and electric catfish possess a genuine organ — built from modified muscle cells called electrocytes — that ordinary motor neurons control exactly as they control any other muscle. The only difference is what the cells do when triggered: instead of contracting to produce force, they discharge to produce a directed electrical output. The mind never touches electricity. It fires a neuron. The neuron is wired to a cell whose job is converting that signal into energy output. This is real, published biology, not an invented workaround — and it maps onto zero energy almost without modification.

**Locked mechanism, universal across every Type:** assimilation doesn't just grant a stored quantity of zero energy — it seeds interface tissue, physically wired into the existing nervous system the same way muscle already is, whose specific job is holding and releasing zero-point potential. The mind controls this tissue exactly as it controls a bicep, by firing a neuron at it. The tissue is what actually touches the energy. Nobody's mind ever commands zero energy directly — not even Eve's own intention-to-PSU-network relationship works any differently in kind, only in degree (hers is deliberately engineered; theirs was granted).

**Storage and recharging use the same structure, not a separate mechanism.** Electrocytes maintain a standing resting potential via continuously-running ion pumps, and discharge it on a triggered signal. Interface tissue does the same: it continuously and passively draws in trace ambient zero energy (the Zero Energy Conservation law's leakage) the way ion pumps continuously top up a resting potential, while voluntary use is a separate, triggered event layered on top of that passive baseline.

**How the tissue differs by Type — and why this, not a separate rule, is what actually produces each Type's locked behavior:**

- **Somatic — fused, not discrete.** Interface tissue fuses directly into existing structural tissue (bone, muscle, skin) rather than forming its own boundary. This is the physical reason Somatic output is always-on with only the rate voluntary: there is no discrete structure to switch off, the same way mitochondria can't be told to simply stop.
- **Metano — folded into neural tissue itself.** The interface is not a separate organ operated by the mind; it's a modification of the mind's own substrate. This is the physical reason self-control and external control run on one shared axis for Metano — the interface tissue and the mind directing it are the same tissue.
- **Kinetic — the one Type with a genuine, discrete organ: "the Core."** A true on/off switch, in either direction of output, requires a structure with a clean boundary and its own dedicated nerve connection — the same reason a real muscle can be fully stopped, because it's a separate, addressable unit. Somatic and Metano's locked behavior actively rules this structure out; Kinetic's locked "fully voluntary in both state and rate" requires it. **Placement: the diaphragm** — the body's literal and functional center, and the one muscle already wired for both continuous involuntary default operation *and* full voluntary override, the same dual nature Kinetic's own control rule needs. Output range and working radius (Telekinesis's control-range delta, Transmutation's ~5m field) are independent of this fixed location, the same way a real electric eel's discharge field extends well beyond its physically localized organ.

**"The Core" — naming and discovery, locked.** Kinetic power is immediately, visibly WMD-adjacent from the moment it manifests — unlike Metano's early-stage inward-only danger, an uncontrolled Kinetic is instantly, outwardly destructive, and instantly a public event. This made the organ's discovery nothing like Metano's dark, hidden, autopsy-driven history: it was studied openly and competitively, by many nations' research bodies simultaneously, converging independently on the same plain, obvious descriptive term rather than any single coinage. No country can be credited with naming it first, because no single country meaningfully got there first. The convergent name — **"the Core"** — sits one word away from Eve's own secret **Zero Core**, an intentional, load-bearing echo nobody in-world would ever notice, since her Core is one of her most tightly held secrets, but the reader would. "Zero" reads as marking hers as the origin instance the whole category was unknowingly named after; the bare, unprefixed "Core" quietly implies a scale that a prefix could sit on, without anyone in-world ever having reason to ask what that prefix means.

**Consequence — the Core is a real, locatable, destroyable structure.** Being observable and destroyable through voluntary medical research is a direct consequence of being a discrete organ rather than fused tissue. Destroying a Kinetic's Core is a genuine, permanent method of ending their power. This gives Kinetic its own distinct danger, a cold mirror to Metano's "the hunted": not *we can't safely touch you* (Metano's problem), but *we know exactly where to cut.*

**Why this tissue needs no developmental pathway, and the deliberate contrast this draws with Eve.** Growing an entirely new organ or tissue network in an adult body, with no surgery, gene therapy, or engineering, is not a separate phenomenon requiring its own explanation — it is covered in full by the beacon event already being defined, precisely, as a genuine miracle: an effect with no antecedent in the local causal chain. This is also the clean, deliberate contrast with Evelyn: her interface (the receptor-gated Power Distribution Unit network) was built — mundane, deliberate, engineered, and in principle technically reproducible. Every Enhanced person's interface was granted — instantaneous, unearned, and permanently unrepeatable. She is the only person on Earth whose relationship to zero energy was ever a project rather than a gift.

## Axis 1 — Type

There is no single, globally-agreed classification body, and there could not plausibly be one — the beacon event was simultaneous, global, and indiscriminate, with no coordinating authority behind it. What exists instead is **informal convergence**: independent nations, militaries, and research bodies, observing enough empowered individuals, tend to arrive at the same rough buckets simply because the underlying patterns are real, regardless of who names them first.

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Somatic** | The power acts on the person's own body — strength, speed, regeneration, physical transformation. |
| **Kinetic/Elemental** | The power projects or manipulates external physical forces — force, fire, ice, gravity-adjacent effects. |
| **Metano** *(formerly "Cognitive" — renamed, easier to say)* | The power opens a mutual neural link to another mind — direct communication, personality suppression, behavior control, memory extraction, and (rarely) involuntary function control. See dedicated section below; this Type has no subtypes. |
| **Vital** | **The only self-imposed Type** — not a physically distinct channel, but a chosen doctrine within Kinetic/Transmutation. See dedicated section below. |
| **Anomalous** | Not a fifth "kind of power" alongside the others — a failure mode of the conversion process itself. See dedicated section below. |

**Somatic and Kinetic were the first two Types informally coined by humans**, simply because they're the most visible/flashy in practice. Their single defining difference is **output direction**: Somatic directs its output inward, into the user's own body; Kinetic directs its output outward, away from the user. Nearly everything else about how the two Types behave — control model, cognitive cost, interface tissue structure (see above), even Hybrid interaction — is a direct consequence of this one distinction, not a separate rule layered on top.

**Only three of the five listed Types are physically distinct channels: Somatic, Kinetic, and Metano.** Anomalous is a failure mode of those three, not a fourth channel (see below). Vital is neither a channel nor a failure mode — it is a doctrine a subset of Kinetic/Transmutation users have chosen for themselves, recognized by everyone (including its own members) as its own Type despite having no separate physical mechanism. Every other Type in this table was named because an outside observer discovered a real physical distinction. Vital is the one exception: its boundary was drawn from the inside, by the people practicing it, around a code rather than a mechanism. This mirrors Metano's own history (originally misclassified as Anomalous before the world understood what it actually was) as a second, ongoing reminder that this taxonomy is a human interpretation layered on top of something that doesn't inherently sort itself this way.

## Somatic — subtype breakdown

**Core mechanic:** output is permanently directed inward and cannot be switched off — a Somatic cannot "power down" to a regular human baseline. Only the *rate* of output is voluntary. This is a direct consequence of the interface tissue's structure (see The Interface, above): fused into existing bone, muscle, and skin rather than forming a discrete, switchable organ, it has no boundary to gate. Because the output never stops, it doesn't just produce raw strength — it inseparably reinforces the entire body's structure (bone density, skin/tissue durability, connective tissue, muscle fiber) as one package, since a Somatic physically could not survive producing enhanced output without the body underneath being upgraded to withstand it. This is why some pure Strength-types can out-muscle Eve outright: her ceiling comes from architecture, theirs from raw biological overbuild. Appearance is cosmetic and tells you nothing reliable about a Somatic's ceiling — a Strength-type can look like an ordinary bodybuilder.

| Subtype | Mechanic | Advantages | Disadvantages | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strength-type** | Output is undirected — "poured" evenly, the most basic expression of the Type. | Sheer destructive power; easiest to control; least cognitive burden. | Crude, predictable movement; low agility/reaction speed; easy to counter; inefficient — poor accuracy can make a powerful hit miss entirely; weakest in a battle of attrition. | Raw output magnitude. |
| **Speed-type** | Output is actively routed to specific muscle groups at will, requiring fine, deliberate control. | Extreme speed and reflexes; fine motor control even at high speed (e.g. a full 180° direction change mid-sprint); unpredictable movement. | Real cognitive load from active targeting; destructive power is lower than Strength-type over a broad area, though equal to it when precisely placed. | Peak G-force survived while self-redirecting momentum (stopping, reversing, cornering) — the same structural reinforcement that grants the output is what lets the best Speed-types survive generating and surviving extreme G under redirection, so this stat is a direct consequence of the same root mechanic as their durability, not a separate trait. |

Somatic is intentionally simple in concept: strength-types and speed-types, loosely "juggernauts and spidermans" — the complexity lives in Kinetic instead.

**Somatic's subtype is trainable, not fixed at conversion.** Unlike Kinetic (below), Strength and Speed are not two different physical mechanisms — they're two ways of routing the same inward output, a specialization along one continuous capability rather than a hard fork. This means a Somatic can deliberately retrain from one subtype toward the other over time. This becomes directly relevant to Hybrid cognitive-load management — see Sousei, below.

## Kinetic/Elemental — subtype breakdown

**Core mechanic:** output is fully voluntary in both state and rate — a Kinetic can switch off entirely. This is a direct consequence of possessing a genuine discrete interface organ, **the Core** (see The Interface, above) — a true switch requires a structure with a clean boundary and its own dedicated nerve connection, which only Kinetic's interface tissue has. The trade is total inaccessibility to their own internal energy; none of their output can ever be directed inward. Both Kinetic subtypes carry substantially higher cognitive burden than either Somatic subtype — Kinetic users are required to be sharp, not just capable.

| Subtype | Mechanic | Advantages / notes | Disadvantages | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Telekinesis** | Output is pure directed kinetic force — a vector. Reads externally as telekinesis, flight (propelling off any available anchor, including the ground), and force-fields (a held layer of kinetic energy). | Offensive/defensive monster at the high end: the best known Telekinetics can propel a grain-of-rice-sized object at bullet speed (it disintegrates on release) or excavate roughly an acre three feet deep, all while airborne. | Precision floors out at roughly grain-of-rice scale no matter how strong the user — finer control than that isn't available at any skill level. | Control-range delta — the gap between the smallest and largest object/area a given user can affect. |
| **Transmutation** | Fine control at the *molecular* level only — never atomic/nuclear. Moving, unbinding, and rebinding molecules costs or releases real energy, exactly as in real bond-breaking/bond-forming chemistry (endo/exothermic), which is what keeps the ability physically honest. **Molecular perception is bundled with the ability, not a separate sense** — the same perceptual channel that allows fine unbind/rebind control also lets a skilled Transmutator directly perceive molecular structure, including reading genetic/cellular information already present in tissue (this is what makes Vital possible — see below). Reads externally as a fusion of "elementalist" and "alchemist" — bursting water from a hand, "vibrating molecules in the air" to generate real heat and produce fire. | True elemental transmutation (turning one element into another, e.g. air into gold) is explicitly off the table — that would be nuclear, not molecular, chemistry, and is treated in-world as both physically absurd to attempt and reputationally taboo. In-world quote to preserve: *"Can't you just create gold from air?" — "Nobody wants to start a nuclear chain reaction just to sell gold."* | Working range is capped (not fixed) at roughly 5m centered on the user, meaning a theoretical maximum working volume around a 10m-diameter sphere — never actually approached in practice. Losing control at the high end is treated in-world as a genuinely world-threatening failure state. | Transmutation agility — how fast a user can execute an unbind/rebind operation. |

**Kinetic's subtype is a forced choice, fixed at conversion — unlike Somatic.** Telekinesis and Transmutation are genuinely different physical mechanisms (vector force vs. molecular-level control), not two routings of one capability. Whichever one a portion assimilates into at the moment of conversion is permanent; there is no retraining from one to the other the way a Somatic can retrain Strength ↔ Speed.

## Vital — the only self-imposed Type

**Not a physical channel. Full stop.** Mechanically, every Vital ability is Transmutation — the same molecular-level unbind/rebind control, the same energy cost per real chemistry, the same ~5m working range, the same bundled molecular perception, the same Core-based interface organ. What makes Vital its own recognized Type is a **chosen doctrine**: a subset of Transmutators who direct their molecular control exclusively toward healing and reconstructing living tissue, under an explicit oath of no harm — a group defined by a code, not a mechanism, and recognized as distinct by everyone, including themselves, on that basis alone.

**Measurement — knowledge is the multiplier, not raw output.** A Vital practitioner's power is a direct function of how completely they understand the structure they're working on. A novice with surgical training can stop bleeding or knit a simple fracture; a master who has spent decades studying human anatomy at every level can direct something as dramatic as regenerating a missing limb. The Transmutation ability itself doesn't change between the two — what changes is which signals they know to place, where, and in what order.

**Why this is plausible without requiring a Vital practitioner to "know DNA" — locked mechanism:**
- **Own-genome orchestration, not invention.** A Vital-Transmutator regenerating tissue is never composing new genetic information from memory or imagination — the patient's complete, correct genome is already physically present in every healthy cell adjacent to the injury. Bundled molecular perception (see Kinetic/Transmutation, above) lets them read and direct that already-existing information, the way a conductor reads an existing score rather than composing one from nothing.
- **Real-world grounding.** Axolotls and salamanders already regrow entire functioning limbs — correctly patterned bone, muscle, nerve, vasculature — using nothing but their own existing genome and a natural process called blastema formation. Humans carry the same genetic program; it's simply dormant past early childhood (a toddler can partially regrow a fingertip; adults mostly can't). A master Vital-Transmutator isn't inventing new biology — they're manually forcing and precisely directing a program the human body already encodes but doesn't normally run.
- **Cancer — locked as a real, earned limit, not a clean cure-all.** Plausible for **early-stage, localized cancer**: a small, contained cell population a skilled practitioner can directly sense and correct or purge one cell at a time. **Implausible even for the most masterful practitioner once a cancer is advanced or metastasized** — at that point the limiting factor isn't the correction technique, it's the sensing/search problem itself: finding and distinguishing every malignant cell across a population dispersed throughout the body is a haystack problem no amount of skill resolves. This keeps "master healer" meaningfully bounded rather than erasing stakes outright.

**Reconstructive and cosmetic application — locked, following directly from the same logic.** A Vital plastic/reconstructive surgeon, whose specialty *is* soft tissue, cartilage, bone, and facial structure specifically, is the best-equipped Vital specialist for reconstructive work in those tissues — and by the same logic, for elective appearance-changing. Cosmetic reshaping is reconstruction aimed at a different goal: the same molecular-level control, redirected toward intention rather than repair.

Three bounds keep this from becoming unlimited, each a direct consequence of rules already locked elsewhere:
1. **Bounded by existing mass.** Transmutation rearranges matter already present — it never creates new matter from nothing (the same rule that ruled out "gold from air"). Reshaping a face means redistributing tissue already there; meaningfully adding new mass (height, build) would require real biological material as input, a far slower and costlier undertaking than a reshape.
2. **Bounded by range and real energy cost.** The ~5m Transmutation range and the real per-bond energy cost (endo/exothermic, same as always) apply in full — a full reconstruction is a genuinely large, metabolically hot operation, not an instant disguise. It reads as planned surgery over hours with real recovery time, not a mid-fight trick.
3. **Bounded by the same understanding-of-the-body skill ceiling.** A practitioner without sufficient mastery risks botching bone structure or nerve routing, exactly as they would attempting limb regeneration below their true skill level.

This makes a highly skilled Vital plastic surgeon a plausible, genuinely dangerous slow-burn identity-change tool for espionage — surgery-grade realism (hours, real cost, real recovery), not shapeshifting magic.

**Pain — locked as an inherent, real cost of nearly all Vital work.** Regeneration and reshaping both necessarily rebuild or disturb the nerve networks running through the affected tissue, and nerves are exactly as physically real and subject to Transmutation's real-chemistry energy cost as any other tissue being worked on. There is no reason nerve tissue would be exempt from that cost while everything else pays it — by default, this hurts, badly.

- **Pain suppression is achievable through the same skill, not a separate power.** Nerve conduction is itself a molecular/ionic process (sodium/potassium channel gating along the axon). A sufficiently skilled Vital-Transmutator can deliberately, temporarily desensitize nerves at the operation site before working — functionally a molecular local anesthetic, using the same control applied to everything else. This is a deliberate additional step, never automatic.
- **Doctrine note.** A true no-harm Vital practitioner treats pain management as part of the oath itself — not just "don't damage the body," but "don't let the process of healing become an injury in its own right."
- **Consequences worth keeping as real story texture:** a rushed or triage-condition procedure by a genuinely competent, well-meaning practitioner can be honestly, unavoidably agonizing — not malice, just reality. An insufficiently skilled practitioner may cause pain purely from lack of mastery, measured on the same understanding-of-the-body axis as everything else Vital. And a forced procedure performed on an unwilling or restrained subject by an indifferent or malicious practitioner becomes a real, specifically horrifying possibility — genuine torture performed with an ability that, in every other hand, is a healing one. This mirrors the same doctrine-vs-capability tension already locked for Metano (a mind-reading power that is terrifying in untrained or malicious hands, and healing in disciplined ones) — worth keeping as a deliberate, mirrored theme across both Types.

## Metano — no subtypes

**Naming.** Originally classified as Anomalous — Metano only became its own recognized Type once enough cases had emerged for them to name themselves. "Metano" is a clipped form of **Metanoia**, the event that defines the group's history (see below) — meaning the name every Metano carries today quietly commemorates an atrocity their founders committed in desperation. This should be treated as intentional, quiet irony, not coincidence, whenever the name comes up in dialogue or narration.

**Core mechanic — the neural link.** A Metano can open a mutual, two-way neural link to any target's mind, regardless of the target's physical defenses — defense is irrelevant to a power that never touches the body at all. This is a direct consequence of the interface tissue's placement (see The Interface, above): folded into neural tissue itself rather than existing as a separate organ, meaning the interface *is* the mind, and the link it opens is a mind-to-mind connection with nothing physical in between. This is why a single trained Metano can decimate an entire fleet of Somatic and Kinetic enhanced without ever revealing their own form, and why Metanos are the single most coveted asset any government can acquire.

**The link's built-in cost.** The link is mutual by nature — the user receives the target's memories unfiltered, including the full weight of any trauma the target carries, all at once. This is not a separate weakness bolted onto the ability; it is the direct, inseparable cost of the mechanism itself. Untrained/early Metanos were destroyed by this involuntarily — a single combat veteran's accumulated battle trauma flooding in all at once was enough to break a person outright. After training, the link becomes voluntary and gateable, which is why ordinary people don't spontaneously dump trauma onto a Metano on the street. This same struggle — surviving what nearly broke the entire founding population — is why trained, surviving Metanos have the most resilient mental capacity of any enhanced group, almost incomparable to Kinetics.

**No subtypes — one unified mechanic.** Unlike Somatic and Kinetic, Metano does not branch. This is a deliberate irony: the most fractured, brutalized, individually broken origin story of any Type produced the single most unified enhanced population on Earth.

**Self-control and external control are the same axis.** A Metano's mastery over their own mind *is* their mastery over others' minds — these are not two separate skills. This is why the rarest and most dangerous use-case, Involuntary Function Control, requires total internal calm, acceptance, and ruthlessness before it can ever be turned outward, and why the only three known practitioners of it are also the only known Metanos capable of turning it on themselves. **These same three individuals are the three legendary Metano — see Metanoia below.** Their mastery of acceptance is not a coincidental second trait; it is the exact quality that let them survive Metanoia in the first place, and the exact quality Involuntary Function Control demands above all else. Both facts trace to the same root.

**Measurement.** A single "proper" Metano's baseline is a 50m radius with no cap on how many subjects within that radius can be held simultaneously. Range can be extended past 50m, but only at the direct cost of subject count — a hard trade-off, not two separate stats. Both numbers, along with an unmeasurable trauma-absorption ceiling, are downstream of one true variable: raw mental capacity. A bigger mental capacity buys more range, more simultaneous subjects, and more absorbed trauma together, not independently — making Metano's core stat conceptually closer to a single hidden attribute than the visible, external numbers the other Subtypes use.

**Use cases (applications of the one link, not separate powers):**
1. Direct voiceless two-way communication — Metano-to-Metano, or Metano-to-ordinary-human alike (the closest real-world equivalent is Eve's own AEGIS neural link, though mechanically unrelated).
2. Personality suppression, partial or complete.
3. Extreme behavior control — up to and including overriding a target's survival instinct entirely.
4. Memory extraction and manipulation.
5. Involuntary function control — direct override of autonomic systems (e.g. stopping a target's own heart muscle) by issuing a command through the link that the target's own nervous system carries out against itself. Confirmed to stay inside Metano's "acts on mind/information" lane rather than borrowing from Vital — the target's own body does the act, not the Metano directly. Known to exactly three living individuals — the three legendary Metano (see below); requires overriding the single strongest resistance a body has, which is why it demands total mastery of one's own mind first.

**Metanoia — the founding atrocity.** **Locked at Year 2 post-beacon** — early enough that hundreds of Metanos had already been hunted and experimented on for a sustained period beforehand, giving the founders real, accumulated desperation before they broke, and cleanly ahead of the first Hybrid case (see Hybrid Timeline below).

A mass-link of the founding Metanos seized the country of Latvia for roughly one minute using Extreme Behavior Control, imprinting a single command on every person physically present — citizen or not — via the link: *"None of you are worthy to live."* A message was broadcast to the rest of the world simultaneously: *"Let it be known that the people of Latvia are no more. It is now Metanoia."* The result was total, indiscriminate self- and mutual annihilation: every enhanced and unenhanced person in the country turned whatever capability they had — Somatic strength, Kinetic force, a soldier's rifle, bare hands — against themselves and each other, before the imprint drove them to suicide regardless. The event was purely geographic, not ideological: it caught anyone physically inside Latvia's borders, citizen or foreigner alike, mirroring the same uncaused, unselective, intent-free nature already locked for the beacon event itself.

**Mechanism — locked as redistribution, not creation.** Metanoia did not generate new zero energy; nothing in this world can (see the Zero Energy Conservation law, `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`). What the founders did was seize and violently redirect zero energy *already held* by everyone and everything present in Latvia — captive and free Enhanced individuals, weapons, infrastructure — into a single, simultaneous, catastrophic act. This is what makes Metanoia the largest human-caused zero-energy event since the beacon itself in total scale, while never once exceeding Evelyn's own peak output at any instant.

**The founders' cost — locked as redistributable, not fixed per person.** Holding that many seized minds simultaneously burns out a Metano's own neurons — this was always the price. Everything has a cost here: when a comrade pushed the three future legendary Metano out of the link moments before the recoil, that act did not simply remove them from harm at no cost to anyone else. It **redistributed their share of the burnout onto everyone who remained in the link**, meaning the rescue increased what the comrade and the rest of the founders had to absorb to cover the gap. The three did not just survive an atrocity they helped commit — they survived it at a price someone else paid twice over on their behalf.

**The three legendary Metano.** The sole survivors of the founding link, saved only by a comrade's sacrifice at greater cost to those who stayed. They carry a uniquely heavy burden: not just grief at what was done to Metanos, but guilt at having been part of what Metanos did — and the singular acceptance capacity required to forgive both. This same capacity for acceptance is what has always made them the only three capable of Involuntary Function Control (see above). Because they are the only Metano proof that this specific, doubled guilt is survivable at all, their word carries unmatched authority within the community — including, critically, how the community as a whole receives Hybrids (see below).

**Why "the hunted."** Metanos combine the two things that make a population catastrophically easy to victimize: they were, early on, mentally and physically the most vulnerable enhanced (no physical protection of any kind, broken by involuntary trauma-absorption before training was understood) — and simultaneously the single most coveted power on the planet by every government that learned what a trained Metano could do. Hundreds were hunted, captured, and experimented on before and around the Metanoia event. Metano is, as a result, the single most dangerous enhanced group in the world: battle-scarred, traumatized, mentally unstable as a population — a tragic irony, given that this same group, approached correctly, may be the one force on Earth capable of creating real peace and unity.

## Hybrids — multi-Type conversion

**Core mechanic:** the beacon event didn't allocate energy *by Type*. It discharged zero energy unselectively, in variable "portions," drawn from the one fixed pool the Zero Energy Conservation law describes. Type is simply what a person's own physiology/psychology does with whatever portion they received — energy itself is unopinionated about its use. A Hybrid is someone whose portion was large enough, or split enough, to assimilate cleanly into more than one of the **three real physical channels — Somatic, Kinetic-Elemental, Metano** — at once, meaning a Hybrid's body grows more than one interface-tissue type simultaneously (see The Interface, above). (Vital is not a fourth channel: a Somatic or Metano individual whose Kinetic side happens to be a Vital practitioner is still, physically, a two-channel Sousei — Vital is a specialization layered on the Kinetic component, never an additional channel counted separately.)

- **Portion size and portion count are independent.** A mono-Type person can still receive a single enormous portion; a Hybrid can receive a comparatively modest portion split across two or three channels. Both are separate rolls of the same uncaused event — this mirrors the "no intent, no selection" principle already locked for the beacon.
- **Fungibility is the actual payoff of being a Hybrid.** A Hybrid can pour their full combined energy budget into a single channel, functionally rivaling a strong mono-Type of similar total portion — or split it for versatility. Versatility is guaranteed; stacked raw output is a choice.
- **No guaranteed correlation to raw output.** Being a Hybrid raises the *ceiling* of what's available to combine, but does not by itself guarantee a stronger measured result than a mono-Type person — see Tier note below, which further limits how confidently this can even be claimed right now.
- **Somatic-Kinetic Sousei case, locked:** there is no "bleed" between the two sides. Somatic stays permanently on regardless of Hybrid status — a Somatic-Kinetic cannot switch off whatever reinforcement/output is already running in their body. Kinetic simply runs as a second, fully voluntary channel alongside it, through its own independent Core. This is the "magic-swordsman" archetype: a body already overbuilt at rest, wielding fully directable outward force on top of it — real fantasy-monster territory, and intentionally so.

**Hybrid emergence timeline — locked.** Assimilating a *second* Type takes meaningfully longer than the original mono-Type awakening, which lands essentially immediately at the beacon event itself. Locked dates:
- **Year 1 (Day 0):** mono-Type awakenings occur, effectively simultaneous with the beacon event.
- **Year 3:** the first confirmed Sousei (two-Type) case emerges — publicly documented, and the real-world precedent that establishes the 生 naming convention.
- **Just before Year 5 (Evelyn's awakening):** the sole Sansei (three-Type) case emerges — **never publicly confirmed as such; the world has no framework to recognize it.** See below.

**Naming — locked.** Following the real-world pattern of phenomena named after their first documented case (Richter, tsunami) rather than invented top-down, the terms are Japanese, tracing to where the first confirmed multi-Type case emerged and was studied. Rather than numeric labels, the naming is meaning-based, rooted in **生 (sei — "life/born")** — deliberately the same root that anchors the Anomalous "miss-birth" framing below, so Hybrid and Anomalous read as two outcomes of one process rather than two unrelated systems.

- **双生 (Sousei)** — "twin-born" (the real word for biological twins) — **locked** as the classification for two-Type cases. Real, confirmed, publicly known.
- **三生 (Sansei)** — echoing *三生の縁 (sanshō no en)*, a real Buddhist idiom for "a bond spanning three lives." By the naming pattern, this is what a three-Type case would be called — **but no case was ever publicly confirmed, so the term was never actually coined into real use.**

**Sansei — the true cap, and the one hidden case.** Three physical channels (Somatic, Kinetic, Metano) is the theoretical maximum any person can stack — there is no fourth channel left to combine, now that Vital is understood to never have been a separate one. Exactly one person alive holds all three simultaneously — the single strongest known enhanced individual on Earth by any measure available to the world's institutions, and the world does not know they exist. When and if they surface, having no confirmed precedent for even a three-stack, the world will not reach for "Sansei" — it will instead classify them as **Anomalous**: the same label worn by the weakest, most broken conversion failures. This mislabeling is deliberate and load-bearing — it means "Anomalous" carries two contradictory truths depending on the case, and the classification system itself has a blind spot exactly where it matters most. This character is intended as Eve's counterpart and should be built with this irony explicit.

### Sousei — the two-axis bottleneck (energy vs. cognitive capacity)

**Energy output and cognitive capacity are two separate, independent axes — locked.** A Sousei's *energy* budget scales with Hybrid status, running roughly double a mono-Type's (illustrative shorthand, not a strict universal ratio — actual portion size still varies per individual, same as any mono-Type). **Cognitive capacity does not scale at all.** It stays capped at the same 100% every mono-Type operates at — becoming a Hybrid raises how much power is available; it does nothing to raise the mind directing it. This gap — roughly double the output, unchanged capacity to manage it — is the real, honest bottleneck on every Sousei build, not an arbitrary rule imposed on top of the system.

**No switching, no toggle, no cooldown.** Every output in this system has already been locked as continuous and graduated (Somatic's rate, Kinetic's state/rate, Metano's own range-vs-subject-count trade-off) — cognitive allocation between two channels works the same way: a single, continuously variable ratio, moving exactly as fast as intention does, with zero cost to reallocating it. **The bottleneck is not that changing the ratio is slow — it's that the two channels' simultaneous demands cannot both exceed the fixed 100% total at once.** A Sousei can run either channel alone at full strength instantly, or split the ratio any way they choose; what no Sousei can do is have both channels *demand* full capacity at the same time.

**Combination viability follows directly from this — locked table:**

| Combination | Cognitive load | Viability | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength–Telekinesis | Low + High | **Favorable** | Strength's near-zero cognitive footprint leaves the full budget free for Telekinesis. |
| Strength–Transmutation | Low + High | **Viable, rarer in practice** | Same favorable load math as above; fewer users simply because Transmutation's ~5m range limits its overall utility, not because of cognitive conflict. |
| Speed–Telekinesis | High + High | **Poor** | Both channels independently demand heavy, deliberate control — genuine, simultaneous overload risk. |
| Speed–Transmutation | High + High | **Better than Speed–Telekinesis** | Still taxed, but offers meaningfully more versatility and use-case range, making it the second-favored real-world choice despite the shared load. |
| Metano–Strength | Low + High (mental-capacity-driven) | **Most favorable Metano pairing** | Strength's near-zero footprint leaves nearly the full budget free for Metano's own capacity-driven stat (range/subject-count/trauma-absorption) — arguably the most efficient Sousei build in the system. |
| Metano–Speed | High + High | **Genuinely taxed, but not weak** | Real simultaneous-demand conflict — but see below: a taxed combination is not a useless one. Also the pairing the Metano community reads as its own "mastery of self is mastery of others" doctrine made flesh, creating real tension between social reverence and personal cost. |
| Metano–Telekinesis | High + High | **The most cognitively overloaded Sousei combination** | Neither side has Strength-type's "free" undirected output to lean on — expect this to be the rarest Metano Hybrid in practice, and the hardest to run both channels of at real strength simultaneously. |
| Metano–Transmutation | High + High | **Weak in combat, exceptional in application** | Combat-taxed for the same reason as above — but produces the best theoretically possible Vital practitioner: Metano's self-control axis gives direct, first-person insight into a patient's own nervous system and psychological state, layered on top of Transmutation's molecular execution. Defined by what it enables, not by raw combat viability. |

**A taxed combination is not a useless one.** Every channel remains fully available; a high-load Sousei simply cannot demand both at full strength at once. This produces a genuinely different playstyle rather than a weaker one — a continuous blender who can run, say, 70/30 while closing distance and fluidly bleed the ratio toward 20/80 the instant an opening appears, with no dead transition moment, since reallocation carries no cost of its own. A Metano-Telekinetic in particular, despite being the single worst combat-load pairing on paper, can dominate at range and still finish with Metano's full weight the instant a target is cornered — a genuinely unpredictable fighter precisely because the ratio never has to sit still long enough to be read.

**Somatic's trainable subtype becomes a real character lever here.** Because Strength ↔ Speed is retrainable (unlike Kinetic's forced, permanent subtype fork — see above), a mono Speed-type who later awakens Kinetic or Metano as their second Type has a concrete, mechanically-grounded reason to deliberately retrain toward Strength: trading away an old specialization to buy back cognitive headroom for managing the new channel. A real, specific arc — someone who used to be fast, and chooses to stop being fast, purely because the math of running two channels at once demands it.

**Recalibration lag is an individual consequence, not a system rule.** Managing double the output through an unchanged 100%-capped mind is a second, harder version of the same adaptation every newly-enhanced person already went through once at mono-awakening — and just as that first adaptation varied person to person, so does this one. Some genuinely struggle: a beat of hesitation, a flinch of lost fine control, real disorientation under combined load. This is explicitly **not** guaranteed or universal — it is mastery-dependent, the same way Metano's own trauma-absorption ceiling or Vital's anatomical understanding are descriptions of individual capability rather than flat numbers. Eve is explicitly not the yardstick for this: she is characterized as a genuine, near-precognitive-tier outlier even before any of this system touches her, and most Sousei should not be expected to adapt as cleanly as she would.

### Metano and Hybrids — social perception

Because a Hybrid necessarily gains their second Type well after their first, *which Type came first* is the real social variable inside the Metano community — not the final combination someone ends up with. Two distinct cases emerge from this, and the community treats them very differently.

**Case 1 — Outsider awakening Metano second.** A Somatic or Kinetic person who later develops Metano arrives at Metano identity carrying years of history, reputation, and loyalty built entirely outside it. The community does not receive this as "one of us, now stronger" — it's someone arriving into Metano identity from outside, whose prior history must be evaluated rather than assumed trustworthy. Given the years of state-sponsored hunting Metanos endured, this case can plausibly include someone who spent those years inside the very institutions that persecuted Metanos, only to awaken into the identity they once helped hunt — an intense, specific story hook, not a general rule.

**Case 2 — Metano awakening a second Type.** A pure Metano who later develops Somatic or Kinetic already lived through the community's defining vulnerability — real, unprotected exposure as a pure Metano — before their second Type ever arrived. They didn't skip what defines the community; they simply outgrew being harmed by it. This reads as unambiguous good news: one of their own, already trusted, now harder to hurt. A Kinetic-Metano in particular is received as the community's own founding philosophy — *mastery of self is mastery of others* — made flesh twice over.

**Locked rule:** trust and belonging track whichever Type awakened first, not the final combination. A late-arriving Metano is received as a graduate of the community regardless of their first Type; a late-arriving second Type is always someone who has to prove themselves, however useful their new power turns out to be.

**Whose word this authority flows through.** Because the three legendary Metano are the community's own proof that impossible acceptance is survivable, their read on both cases carries unmatched weight — other Metanos follow their lead on trusting Case 1 and celebrating Case 2 not out of blind deference, but because these three have already demonstrated, at the highest possible cost, that the acceptance being asked of the community is something a Metano can actually carry.

## Anomalous — failed conversion, two faces

**Root cause:** the same zero energy, the same beacon — but the conversion into a usable Type never completed properly. A partial, malformed assimilation, including a partial or malformed growth of the underlying interface tissue itself (see The Interface, above) — the physical substrate for directing zero energy exists incompletely or incorrectly, which is as good a candidate physical description of "failed conversion" as any in this document. This is not a "weird powers" catch-all; it is a specific physical failure mode, and the world's instinct to see Anomalous individuals as *weak* or *defective* is largely correct from the outside, even though the underlying cause (an interrupted "miracle") is identical to every successful case. (Note: Metano was itself once classified here, before enough cases emerged for it to be recognized as its own stable Type — a reminder that this bucket's boundary has moved before and may move again.)

The failure resolves toward one of two extremes:

**Low end — nonfunctional conversion.** The power exists but cannot be reliably accessed, controlled, or sustained. Includes would-be Hybrids whose multiple portions never integrated — rather than gaining versatility, they get instability or total inaccessibility across all channels they should have received.

**High end — conversion resolved outside the three channels entirely.** Instead of acting on body, matter, or mind directly, the result acts on *the system itself* — reading outcome-space, nullifying or stealing another person's conversion, amplifying someone else's output, or transferring/granting a portion of one's own energy to another at a cost to the giver. These read as metaphysical precisely because they don't fit anywhere else in the taxonomy.

**Flagship high-end case — Foresight.** Exactly one person alive holds genuine future-sight–flavored ability. Mechanically, this stays inside the existing metaphysics lock: it is not a real window into other branches, but an inferential/pattern-extrapolation capability so far beyond anything Metano's mind-reading can do that it is indistinguishable from prophecy to everyone who witnesses it — very possibly including the bearer. The ability carries a cost paid in the bearer's own life. **Exact cost mechanism not yet locked** — see Open Questions. **How this gets explained to Eve (and the reader) is also not yet built** — flagged as a dedicated future task, not solved in this pass.

## Axis 2 — Tier

> **⚠️ UNDER CONSTRUCTION — DO NOT REFERENCE.** Tier is frozen pending a decision on whether it survives at all (see Open Questions). Nothing below should be treated as locked story fact until this banner is removed.

**Core principle (as previously developed, now paused):** the empowerment itself cannot be measured, because it's genuinely uncaused. What *could* be measured is observable energetic output — work, force, heat, mass displaced per unit time — via the same calorimetry/force-measurement instrumentation already used elsewhere in this world. The scale shape explored was logarithmic (Richter-like), explicitly to avoid a linear "Class E through S" feel.

**Why it's paused:** Type is already one layer of human interpretation imposed on an inherently unexplained phenomenon. Tier would be a second, even more arbitrary layer — a human *measurement standard* bolted onto a human *naming standard*. Given that, building further mechanics on top of Tier right now risks locking in structure that may need to be torn out later.

**Emerging replacement candidate:** the per-Subtype measurement axes now being built (raw output for Strength, peak G-force for Speed, control-range delta for Telekinesis, transmutation agility for Transmutation, anatomical-mastery depth for Vital, combined range/subject-count for Metano) may end up replacing Tier entirely, rather than Tier being reconstructed on top of Type. Each axis is *relevant to its own Type/Subtype specifically*, rather than one universal number forced across fundamentally different kinds of output — which may be the more physically honest solution to the exact problem that froze Tier in the first place. Not yet decided; flagged for a future session.
