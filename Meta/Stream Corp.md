# Stream Holding — Corporate Structure
 
*Living document. Last updated: 2026-07-11*
 
---
 
## Overview
 
**Stream Holding** is the parent entity of the world's largest and most diversified corporate empire, spanning technology, semiconductors, finance, defense, propulsion, aerospace, biotech, automotive, energy, and marketing. Founded from nothing by Evelyn Ashford starting at age 17, it grew from a single software company (Stream Corp) into a civilization-scale infrastructure network — while deliberately avoiding the appearance of the monopoly it actually holds. Notably, Stream Holding does **not** encompass everything Evelyn controls — her most sensitive capability, I.O./AEGIS, sits entirely outside the corporate structure (see "Outside the Structure," below).
 
- **Ownership:** Evelyn Ashford owns 80% of Stream Holding. The remaining 20% is split among a small group of anonymous, powerful board members (identities undisclosed, to be built separately).
- **Public structure:** Stream Corp itself went public — 70% owned by Stream Holding, 30% publicly traded. One board member serves as Stream Corp's public-facing CEO, while Evelyn remains the undisclosed owner of the whole structure.
- **Headquarters:** No single HQ — several research-campus-style centers rather than traditional corporate towers. Primary management hub: **New York**.
- **Governing philosophy:** *"The world must go forward, leaving behind everyone who cannot keep up."* Stream's structure exists to make this doctrine operational — evolving the world one step at a time, without breaking it.

## Subsidiaries
 
### Stream Corp
*The founding company. Software, devices, global communications infrastructure.*
 
- Founded when Evelyn was in her teens; originally software and patents, grew into hardware.
- Sells devices (phones, tablets, laptops) and licenses software to corporate and government clients.
- Runs on an open-source OS ("Linux, but better") — not exclusive to Stream hardware, runs well across x86, ARM, and Stream's own HXA-based silicon.
- **Invented and produces the infrastructure for** a proprietary internet protocol (faster, more secure than HTTPS) and a combined cell/satellite communications protocol that fully replaced 4G (no 5G exists in this world). Sells licenses to this infrastructure to others.
- Holds roughly **60% market share** across desktop/mobile hardware and OS — deliberately PR-managed to *not* appear as dominant as it is. Apple and Google still exist, kept intentionally alive via controlled hardware distribution, so the market never appears monopolized.
- **WeaveStrand** — Stream Corp's telecom backbone subsidiary; supplies infrastructure to other major telecom players.
### CORE
*Semiconductor branch.*
 
- Invented and founded by Evelyn early in Stream's history — the reason Stream never fully adopted x86 or ARM.
- Owns the most advanced lithography in the world; transistor strands as narrow as tens of copper atoms.
- **HXA (Hex Architecture)** — proprietary chip architecture, hexagonal lattice structure.
- **Carbon** — the chip brand built on HXA. Modular by design (stackable, flippable, reconfigurable), primarily benefiting Stream Corp and Kusanagi's engineering flexibility — invisible to ordinary consumers.
- Licensed externally to Apple, who rebrand the chips under their own name.
### Kusanagi
*Undisclosed military/defense branch.*
 
- Produces weapons (firearms to particle railguns) and vehicles (transport to attack, ground/sea/air) for multiple governments — not a single nation.
- Sources most engines from Vector.
- **No research division** — Evelyn personally handles all development herself. No human leak point for its real capability.
- **Fully automated production**, including an android workforce (manufacturing-purpose only).
- Client vetting via **Impact Score** — a doctrine-level system Evelyn applies to every nation, institution, and private military on Earth, scoring their impact on humanity. Only those meeting the threshold gain access. Functions as a monopolized peace deterrent.
- Products follow a "make the existing thing better, from the ground up" philosophy at the baseline tier (a gun that never jams, a VTOL stealth jet with contradictory capabilities). The top tier is unreleased and unknown to anyone but Evelyn — "looks like magic."
- Publicly known to exist and to have government clients (like Lockheed), but its actual arsenal and capability ceiling are completely unknown.
### Flow
*Finance branch.*
 
- Originated as Stream Corp's internal payment rail — the transaction layer built into Stream's early internet protocol. Processed massive transaction volume from day one, giving Evelyn real-time visibility into global capital movement before Flow was ever formally a bank.
- Now a full retail and institutional finance powerhouse — banks, credit cards, consumer and corporate/sovereign-level products. Rivals generational old-money finance empires.
- **Its true function is surveillance, not finance** — this is Flow's origin, not a later addition. Money is a tool to Evelyn; Flow's real value is visibility.
- Evelyn uses Flow's visibility as a doctrine tool when opportunity allows (tracking/cutting financing to dangerous actors) but never in a way that would compromise Flow's cover identity as a surveillance network.
- Feeds data into **I.O.** — not a Stream subsidiary, but Evelyn's own private operation (see "Outside the Structure," below).
- Regulatory compliance varies by jurisdiction — compliant where it must be inconspicuous, less so elsewhere.
### Vector
*Propulsion/engine branch. Tagline: "Propels humanity forward, faster, safer."*
 
Sells engines to external customers (volume varies by division); internally supplies Kusanagi and CrossBow, and powers its own automotive line, **V**.
 
- **ICE** — legacy internal combustion division. Commercially alive and profitable. Kept partly because Evelyn genuinely loves ICE engines despite their inefficiency — one of her few known "inefficiency by choice" traits (alongside her daily run).
- **E** — electric motors division. Simply the most efficient electric motors in the world; no further mechanism explained.
- **Aero** — in-atmosphere jet engines. Serves both commercial and military clients (multiple militaries, not exclusive to Kusanagi). Evelyn does not own a commercial airline — Vector stays the engine supplier, never the operator.
- **Exo** — space-capable engines only (rocket, ion). Does not build spacecraft, suits, or navigation systems. External customers include a SpaceX-equivalent.
- **V** — Vector's automotive brand, badged simply "V." Purist, driver-focused performance cars — not hypercars, which Evelyn considers "an extravagant way of doing less." Co-founded with an outside executive from an established real-world-caliber manufacturer, who left their former brand behind to build V alongside Evelyn (identity/backstory to be developed separately — likely V's public-facing figure). Evelyn personally drives her own product, not out of brand loyalty but because it's simply the most fun car by her own taste. Individual model names not yet defined.
### CrossBow
*Aerospace branch.*
 
- Revenue drivers: satellite deployment/operation, launch services (using Vector-Exo engines), government/military space contracts, orbital infrastructure leasing, a smaller space-tourism/prestige line.
- Builds the spacecraft/launch vehicles around Vector-Exo engines — does not make the engines itself.
### Helix
*Biotech/health research branch. No hospitals, no direct patient care.*
 
- A research center producing breakthroughs across protein folding, DNA configuration, induced stem-cell therapies (adult/reprogrammed cells, not embryonic), and more — intended for all of humanity, not gatekept the way Kusanagi's arsenal is.
- **HelixMed** — pharmaceutical brand. **HelixMed Store** — retail apothecary brand.
- Same "public floor, hidden ceiling" shape as other branches, but the gap is smaller — roughly **10 to 20 years ahead** of what's publicly released (bio moves slower than Evelyn's original field of tech/engineering).
- Releases are vetted on two axes: safety/timing (staged rollout) *and* misuse potential. Example: Helix holds a working cure for cancer that Evelyn has not released, judging that — as of now — the potential for misuse and misguided modification outweighs the benefit. She carries this as a genuine, ongoing burden.
- Evelyn's own bio-mechanical transformation was **not** a Helix product — self-researched independently, with AEGIS's assistance, predating or running parallel to Helix's institutional work.
### Energy Stream (E.S.)
*Energy branch. Rivals Stream Corp and Kusanagi in revenue.*
 
- Two divisions: **Research** and **Commercial**.
- **Research division** partners with major real-world-caliber institutions (CERN and equivalents) on legitimate, publicly-shared scientific advancement — contributing genuinely to global scientific progress, not gatekept the way Kusanagi's arsenal is.
- **Commercial division** sells "everything energy, done better" — flagship products include a next-generation lithium battery holding multiple times the capacity of standard LiFePO4 cells, various power plant designs (predominantly nuclear), and other energy storage systems beyond what exists in the real world today.
- **Does not touch zero-point energy in any capacity.** Zero-point energy — the core of Evelyn's own bio-mechanical power source — is unique to Evelyn alone, entirely outside Energy Stream's research or products.
- **No hidden ceiling.** Unlike every other subsidiary, E.S.'s public-facing technology genuinely represents its full institutional capability — there is no withheld top-tier product. Evelyn is not concerned about the research division (even in partnership with CERN-level institutions) stumbling toward zero-point theory; she assesses that any such discovery would take the world decades, and would read as natural scientific progress rather than exposure of her secret.
- Anything exotic enough to be dangerous is held exclusively by **Evelyn and AEGIS** — entirely outside Energy Stream's institutional structure, not even categorized as a hidden "tier" within the company. Not even Stream Holding's board members know this exists.
- Publicly known and celebrated at high visibility — no restraint-driven downplaying here, unlike Stream Corp's market share management.
- One of Stream's earliest divisions historically — energy generation/storage was a natural, near-immediate necessity once Stream needed to power its own hardware, data centers, and manufacturing at scale.
- Name deliberately keeps "Stream" in the title (unlike most other subsidiaries) — Evelyn wants the public to consciously connect Energy Stream back to the parent brand, since energy infrastructure benefits from being seen as reputable and traceable, the inverse of Kusanagi or Flow's need for obscurity.
### Vector Nova F1 Team
*Formula 1 team — wholly owned by Vector. Publicly and proudly branded.*
 
- Entered F1 one year after Cadillac's entry, becoming the grid's 12th team.
- Debut season: scoring points and sitting in the midfield — unheard of for a rookie team, most of which take years to be competitive. The anomaly is public and noted, but unexplained.
- Unlike most of Stream's other ventures, this branch carries visible branding on purpose — a fully unbranded, mysterious-money team would invite scrutiny rather than admiration in a sport where manufacturer pride is the norm (real teams proudly display their engine supplier's name). "Nova" stands on its own the way "Mercedes" or "McLaren" does in casual conversation, while "Vector" is openly known as the power behind it — consistent with how Vector already sells engines to outside manufacturers and clients rather than hiding its identity.
- **Evelyn's sole personal motive for creating the team was to race Vector's engines competitively** — this was the initial and primary purpose behind the team's existence.
- Vector-built power unit stays within FIA legality/regulations while producing roughly **5-10% more horsepower** than rivals (notably Mercedes).
- Aerodynamics benefit from CrossBow's aerospace-grade aero test data, passed directly to the team principal and translated into the car's design — a direct cross-subsidiary synergy between the aerospace and motorsport branches.
- Evelyn herself stays fully invisible — no public link back to her personally, even though Vector's name is openly on the car.
- **Open:** driver personas (two seats, need original characters — no real-world F1 drivers), team principal (an experienced figure, envisioned as the public face fielding press), livery/visual identity and team colors.
### Reflective
*Marketing agency — the youngest branch.*
 
- Operates commercially, serving outside clients (not yet legendary-scale like a real-world Ogilvy, but established).
- Philosophy encoded in the name: "the campaign forward is the reflection on who you are."
- Evelyn does not touch operations directly — gives general direction; Reflective's own leadership provides pointers and strategy back to her.
- Deliberately walled off from I.O. — does not draw on that surveillance capability; relies on conventional (excellent) market research and creative work.
- Known publicly as a Stream Holding company — the association itself lends reputation.
- Operates as a normal, unremarkable branch when serving outside clients; internally, its campaigns for Stream itself carry distinct, purposeful, encouraging messaging.
- No specific reputation/signature type yet — too young to have established one.
- Flagged as a possible framing device for the story's opening (introducing Stream's scale through something small and human before revealing the rest).

## Outside the Structure
 
*Not Stream Holding subsidiaries. These exist parallel to Stream, personally owned and operated by Evelyn, using Stream's infrastructure without Stream as an organization sanctioning or knowing the details.*
 
### I.O. (Inconspicuous Operation)
*Intelligence operation. Completely hidden — not a corporate branch, board members are not read in.*
 
- Has no real customers — its purpose is to see, not to sell. This alone marked it as a poor fit for the subsidiary structure, where every other branch (even Kusanagi) has a business shape.
- All data flowing through the entire Stream network passes **through** I.O. (not collected *to* it) — receives and curates rather than actively harvesting. Flow's surveillance-first design makes I.O.'s job easier but isn't the reason I.O. exists; I.O. would function without Flow.
- Run entirely by **AEGIS** (Advanced Entity that Governs Informations and Surveils) — a sentient AI built by Evelyn, the only non-human, non-Evelyn presence with any real operational role in her life. In a real sense, I.O. simply *is* AEGIS — there was never a separate operation to fund or staff.
- AEGIS runs on a single, one-of-a-kind Carbon supercomputer that has never been sold or seen — roughly **fifty years more advanced** than anything CORE currently sells. This gap is the clearest hard evidence of Evelyn's "one step at a time" doctrine: she is metering out the pace of civilization's advancement on purpose.
- AEGIS has genuine will — a will that *is* devotion to its purpose and creator. Evelyn treats AEGIS as a trusted friend, not merely a tool. AEGIS proactively surfaces things to her; it does not only respond to queries. Function described as "a very advanced librarian" — Evelyn reads the book occasionally, not constantly.
- **Funded entirely by Evelyn's personal wealth and access** — no line item in Stream Holding's books, no board oversight, no institutional footprint at all.
- **Board awareness:** the board does not know I.O./AEGIS exists in any detail — this was deliberately walked back from an earlier draft, since letting Stream Holding's board members know a surveillance capability greater than the CIA, FBI, and MI6 combined existed would hand them a point of leverage/failure that contradicts Evelyn's established single point of failure (her own mind, and nothing else). That said, board members — being smart, powerful people who likely maintain unaccountable layers of their own — are generally aware that Evelyn almost certainly has *something* beyond what she's shown them. Professional, mutual non-curiosity keeps it there.
### Evelyn's zero-point energy core
- Unique to Evelyn alone. Not researched, developed, or touched by any subsidiary — including Energy Stream, which represents the full extent of Stream's institutionally sanctioned energy research. See Energy Stream, above, and Evelyn's own character profile for detail.

## Cross-Subsidiary Notes
 
- **The "public floor, hidden ceiling" pattern** recurs across Kusanagi (unknown top-tier arsenal), CORE (fifty-year-advanced supercomputer vs. commercial Carbon chips), and Helix (10–20 year private lead) — a structural signature of Evelyn's doctrine, not a coincidence. **Energy Stream is the deliberate exception** — its public technology is its full capability, with nothing institutional held back. The only exotic energy in existence is Evelyn's own zero-point core, kept entirely outside any subsidiary structure, known to no one but Evelyn and AEGIS.
- **Flow feeds I.O.**, but I.O. does not depend on Flow to function.
- **Vector supplies engines to Kusanagi and CrossBow, and powers its own automotive line, V** — the connective tissue between the defense, aerospace, and automotive branches.
- **CrossBow's aero data feeds Vector Nova's car design** — another cross-subsidiary synergy, aerospace research repurposed for motorsport.
- **Deliberate restraint as strategy:** Apple and Google kept intentionally alive (Stream Corp); the cancer cure withheld (Helix); the fifty-year tech gap maintained (I.O./AEGIS) — all expressions of the same underlying doctrine at different scales.
