# Post-Beacon World State — The Five Missing Years

*Living document. Last updated: 2026-09-06*

---

## Overview

This document tracks the state of the world during the five-year gap between the beacon event (the story's prologue — see `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`, Tier 5) and Evelyn's eventual return. It is separate from the metaphysics document because its content is sociopolitical and corporate, not cosmological — but every thread here is a direct, traceable consequence of that single event. Intended to grow further as later threads (geopolitics/government response, gates and monster containment status, the Type/Tier system's real-world adoption) are built out.

## AEGIS — The Search That Never Ends

- AEGIS's search for Evelyn continues for the full five years and **never locates her.** The Arctic black site's isolation (already established as deliberately signal-hardened, plus the terrain plausibly altered by her own discharge) defeats every search AEGIS can mount using Evelyn's personal, off-books assets — the only resources AEGIS can use without exposing I.O.'s existence to Stream's board or the wider world.
- **AEGIS refuses to accept Evelyn's death.** When Stream Holding's board internally declares her dead (see below), AEGIS does not concur. This is consistent with AEGIS's established characterization as possessing genuine will — devotion to Evelyn as its purpose and creator, not a probability engine reporting the statistically likely outcome. AEGIS's continued search and continued belief she is alive is a standing, private act of loyalty no one else shares or knows about.
- AEGIS carries, alone, for five years: certainty that Evelyn is alive, inability to prove it to anyone, inability to find her, and the operational discipline to keep Stream's and I.O.'s secrets intact regardless.

## Stream Holding — The Five-Year Governance Arc

**Phase 1 — Paralysis (immediate aftermath).** No instruction, no successor, no legal mechanism for the board to act on Evelyn's 80% stake while she is merely unreachable rather than confirmed dead. The board's only real move is defensive: maintain existing operations, suppress panic, avoid triggering a stock collapse or inviting antitrust/nationalization attention prematurely. Nothing new is greenlit. Every "hidden ceiling" program (Kusanagi's top-tier arsenal, Helix's withheld cures, CORE's most advanced silicon) goes fully dark — none of it can be authorized, advanced, or even fully understood by anyone left standing.

**Phase 2 — Declared dead (board-internal, timing to be fixed in a future session).** Under sustained pressure — legal, financial, and the sheer impossibility of running a company this size on indefinite limbo — the board internally declares Evelyn Ashford dead. This is a private/internal declaration, not necessarily a public one (open question — see below), intended to unlock whatever legal mechanisms (presumed-death statutes, estate succession) might let the board finally act with real authority over her stake. **AEGIS does not accept this declaration** — the one dissenting voice, unheard by anyone empowered to listen.
- **Open complication worth building out:** Evelyn, consistent with never having planned for her own permanent absence, most likely left no will and no named heir for her 80% stake. A declaration of death doesn't cleanly resolve anything — it may simply trade one crisis (paralysis) for another (a contested, unresolved inheritance battle over the most valuable and dangerous ownership stake on Earth), with courts, competing claimants, and government interest all entering the picture. Not yet locked as to who claims what, or how far this escalates.

**Phase 3 — The Takeover (locked).** Invoking wartime/national-security emergency powers (justified by the active gate crisis and the year-5 war), the **US government takes full operational control of every Stream Holding branch — Stream Corp, CORE, Kusanagi, Flow, Vector, CrossBow, Helix, Energy Stream, Reflective, all of it — with one single exception: I.O.** I.O. survives untouched not through resistance or concealment effort, but simply because it was never part of the corporate structure the board (or now the government) knew to seize — Evelyn's secrecy doctrine holds by omission, not defense. **Legal ownership of Evelyn's 80% stake remains formally unresolved** — the Phase 2 death declaration and any inheritance dispute are effectively parked, superseded by operational seizure rather than settled by it. The government runs the empire; who legally owns it stays an open question underneath that.
- **Control without capability — the critical catch.** Seizing Kusanagi does not unlock Kusanagi's true arsenal — the hidden top tier remains "unreleased and unknown to anyone but Evelyn," per its original design specifically to have no human leak point. The same bottleneck plausibly applies wherever Evelyn's personal, undocumented expertise was the actual ceiling (CORE's most advanced fabrication, Helix's most withheld work). **The US gains ownership and the baseline-tier capability, not the capability that would have actually changed the balance of power.** All the takeover practically achieves is symbolic deterrence — flaunting Kusanagi's already-public baseline tech as a show of strength — not a genuine military leap.
- **This produces a locked, MAD-style global equilibrium.** Every major power now understands the others could, in principle, be devastated by what any one of them might already control or eventually unlock — and none of them can verify how much of Evelyn's true capability the US actually has access to. This uncertainty is itself the deterrent: **everyone has the potential to decimate everyone else, and precisely because no one can confirm the real ceiling, no one moves.** This sharpens, rather than resolves, the Tier 1 cold-war dynamic already locked in the Political Landscape section below.

## The World's Collective Amnesia

- Evelyn's disappearance does **not** become the defining crisis of the five years, despite her being (per her own established public profile) the owner of the world's largest tech empire and, functionally, the person holding much of the world's infrastructure together. She is overshadowed almost immediately by the more immediate, visible, and universally threatening crisis of gates, monsters, and the enhanced.
- **Most of the world simply forgets her**, or files her disappearance as one unresolved mystery among many, once faced with existential, doorstep-level threats. This is not indifference written as a flaw — it's the same "the world moves forward, leaving behind what it can no longer prioritize" logic her own doctrine describes, now applied to her by the world she built it for.
- **A small dark-web community treats her disappearance as a conspiracy theory** — connecting it to the beacon event, correctly, but with no way to prove it and no broader traction. Fringe, unprovable, dismissible.
- **Governments privately suspect a connection** between her disappearance and the timing of the beacon event, but lack any way to substantiate or act on the suspicion — consistent with the metaphysics document's established rule that the event leaves no traceable causal chain. Preoccupation with the primary crisis leaves little institutional appetite to pursue it further even where suspicion exists.
- **Only AEGIS holds full, confident knowledge** — that she caused it, that she is alive, and that she cannot be found — and has both the motive (loyalty) and the standing directive (Evelyn's own secrecy doctrine, upheld even in her absence) to never reveal any of it.

## World Timeline — Beacon Event to Year 5

*Global events, distinct from the Stream/AEGIS/Evelyn-specific threads above, though they unfold in parallel and increasingly intersect (see Stream Phase 3, below).*

### Day 0 — The Beacon
Gates open simultaneously worldwide — **not uniformly distributed**, but densest near the Arctic (the beacon's point of origin) and attenuating with distance, consistent with a pressure-equalization event radiating outward from its source (see `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`, Tier 4). Monsters pour through the most volatile gates immediately, causing real, immediate casualties and destruction with zero warning. The empowerment shockwave lands on scattered individuals globally in the same instant, but its effects are not immediately legible — it reads first as mass hysteria or an unexplained medical phenomenon, not "powers." Markets crash; aviation and shipping seize up; every government declares emergency measures independently. No coordinated global response exists yet — nothing like this has ever required one.

### Weeks 1–4 — First contact, in all four flavors
- First confirmed monster engagements: militaries lose early skirmishes, since conventional weapons perform unevenly across an as-yet-unmeasured Tier range.
- First peaceful contact with a "more intelligent, less hostile" arrival occurs somewhere — whichever government reaches this first gains outsized geopolitical leverage over the global narrative.
- Elsewhere, a genuinely hostile, intelligent incursion reads far more like an invasion than a disaster.
- Empowered individuals begin discovering their abilities under real duress — many early deaths from people who don't yet know what they can or can't survive, alongside a few accidental, instant local heroes.

### Months 1–12 — Scrambling toward structure
Martial law in gate-adjacent zones. An international summit attempts a unified global response and fails to produce a real global authority (consistent with `Fantasy_World_Power_System.md`'s "no single classification body" rule). Type/Tier classification begins emerging independently from military and scientific communities who need a working shorthand immediately. Refugee crises form around active gates. The first visionary/"prophet" figures and doomsday cults appear, each claiming a fragment of understanding about what actually happened (see the still-open "fragment of the picture" visionary thread in the metaphysics document).

### Years 1–3 — Adaptation, and the world turns pragmatic and ugly
Nations begin actively recruiting or conscripting empowered individuals — an overnight new class of geopolitical asset. Gate zones harden into semi-permanent militarized no-man's-lands; some become research installations, others fully lawless frontiers. Black markets emerge around whatever crosses through. Private military contractors begin operating in gate zones alongside or instead of national armies. This period directly intersects with Stream's governance collapse (see Phase 3, above) — governments increasingly desperate for exactly the hidden-tier defense technology Stream can no longer authorize is what drives escalating seizure attempts on Kusanagi specifically.

### Years 3–5 — An uneven, fragile "new normal"
**Containment is genuinely mixed, not uniform.** Wealthy, stable nations have largely brought gate zones under control — militarized, monitored, functionally managed. Poorer or less stable regions have not — gate zones there remain lawless frontiers, contested by monsters, private actors, black markets, and in some cases hostile intelligent factions that never left. This unevenness becomes its own geopolitical fault line: safety and access to "the new normal" is not equally distributed, and it maps uncomfortably well onto existing global inequality. Monsters and gates become a fact of life rather than front-page news in the contained regions, while remaining an active, ongoing catastrophe in the uncontained ones. Empowered individuals now occupy real cultural roles — celebrities, military assets, or persecuted minorities, unevenly by region. Type/Tier is in wide but non-standardized use.

### Year 5 — Evelyn wakes into an active, major crisis
**Locked: she does not wake into a lull.** Something significant is actively unfolding at the moment her recovery reaches the floor sufficient for consciousness — she returns directly into the middle of an ongoing acute event, not a world that has settled. **The specific nature of this crisis is not yet chosen** — candidate directions include (not yet decided, flagged for a future session): a major hostile-intelligent-being incursion breaching a previously contained zone; a catastrophic failure/escalation at a lawless frontier gate; or a Stream-adjacent crisis (e.g., the Phase 3 seizure conflict over Kusanagi reaching a breaking point) colliding directly with the gate/monster crisis. Whichever is chosen should ideally connect to more than one existing thread rather than introducing an isolated new one.

## Humanity's Reaction — Gates, Enhanced, and Arrivals

*Detail layer for the timeline above, broken out by subject rather than by year, since each track develops on its own arc.*

### Gates
- **Day 0–Month 1:** pure incomprehension — no framework yet, so explanations range from alien invasion to secret government experiment to divine judgment. Some conspiracy theories point toward advanced-tech origins without knowing how close to true that is. Military lockdowns go up around every gate with no standardized protocol.
- **Year 1:** legitimate physics research (plausibly surfacing independently through CERN-equivalent institutions under crisis pressure, converging on the same wormhole/negative-energy-density theory already underlying Folder tech) gives humanity real, if crude, understanding of *what* a gate is, without ever learning *why* they appeared. A genuine silver lining: human science legitimately catches up to something Evelyn was quietly decades ahead on.
- **Years 1–3:** gates get informally classified **by outcome, not cause** — "hot" (monster-producing), "dormant," and rare "contact" gates leading to peaceful or resource-rich worlds. No single naming convention emerges, mirroring the same organic convergence already locked for Type/Tier.
- **Years 3–5:** patchwork sovereignty disputes — nations claim gates on their territory as strategic resources; gates in contested/international spaces become genuine flashpoints. A UN-equivalent treaty attempt on gate access/ownership largely fails to gain universal traction, consistent with the "no real global authority" rule. Gate-adjacent land becomes either worthless (near hot gates) or a boomtown (near contact/resource gates).

### Enhanced
*(Author's chosen in-world term for empowered individuals — used going forward in place of "empowered.")*
- **Weeks 1–Year 1:** genuine, ugly fear precedes any framework — mob violence against the newly-Enhanced, mistaken for contagion, mutation, or possession, before anyone has language for what's happening to them.
- **Year 1 onward:** governments pivot fast once strategic value becomes obvious. Authoritarian states move toward forced conscription; wealthier democracies toward incentive-based recruitment or licensing regimes. Genuine legal patchwork results — some nations pass Enhanced-rights legislation, others impose registration and monitoring or worse.
- **Years 2–5:** real social stratification sets in. Enhanced individuals become a privileged new elite in some regions, a controlled or persecuted minority in others. High-Tier individuals function as de facto strategic assets — a handful of extremely high-Tier people meaningfully altering global power balance, roughly analogous to how nuclear arsenals reshape deterrence. Commercial culture adapts (competitive leagues, entertainment built around lower-Tier Enhanced), while most Enhanced people are simply trying to survive a power they never asked for — some organize into support communities, others into exploitative gangs.

### What comes through the gates
- **Monsters:** treated purely as a threat-and-resource problem. Military doctrine develops around Tier-based culling operations; "monster hunting" becomes a formal profession and eventually a military branch. Their biology becomes a real resource economy — materials, organs, and compounds valuable enough to draw serious Helix-adjacent biotech interest.
- **More intelligent, less hostile arrivals:** the genuinely novel diplomatic problem. Some integrate, raising real legal-personhood and immigration-equivalent questions humanity has no precedent for; others remain isolated and watchful. Xenophobia coexists uneasily with cautious alliance-building.
- **Intelligent, hostile arrivals — the real wars of this era.** Unlike monster culling, these are organized, sustained conflicts against coordinated non-human forces, not disaster response.

### Year 5 crisis — locked
A hostile intelligent faction establishes a genuine, sustained territorial foothold in one of the "lawless frontier" regions (see the mixed-containment split, above). By year 5, this escalates into open, large-scale conflict — **the opening of a long war between humanity and this hostile faction**, not a contained incident. This is the acute crisis Evelyn wakes into: not a single battle to resolve, but the beginning of a war already underway, connecting her return directly to the frontier/containment thread already built rather than introducing an isolated new threat.

## The Earth's Wound — Geophysical Consequences

*See `Trans-Dimensional_Metaphysics.md`, Consequence 5, for the underlying mechanism (energy conservation: the Earth absorbed a share of the discharge as heat and mechanical stress, concentrated at the intersection of Arctic proximity and pre-existing geological weak points).*

- **Volcanic reactivation.** Dormant systems near the Arctic and along major fault lines return to activity — Iceland (genuinely volcanic, genuinely Arctic-proximate) is a natural early candidate. Possible emergence of volcanic activity in locations with no prior history.
- **Seismic uptick.** Increased earthquake frequency and intensity concentrated along existing fault lines rather than distributed randomly worldwide.
- **Charged-particle/ionization zones.** Localized atmospheric and ground-level effects, potentially including unusual aurora-like phenomena at unexpected latitudes — and a second, geology-based explanation for electromagnetic dead zones, alongside deliberately hardened black sites (see the prologue).
- **Regional climate anomalies.** Pressure and temperature shifts severe enough to functionally reshape small areas — pockets of geothermal warmth within Arctic ice, or destabilized temperate zones elsewhere — unintended small-scale terraforming.
- **Geopolitical fallout — significant, not yet built out.** A nation gaining sudden volcanic or seismic activity (or, conversely, losing stability it relied on) is a major geopolitical event in its own right, layered on top of the Arctic reshuffle and golden-gate dynamics already locked below. Likely intersects directly with the political landscape — worth building out alongside it.
- **Thematic note:** the Earth carries the same wound Evelyn does, at planetary scale — a second body scarred by the identical event, plausibly on its own slow recovery arc in parallel with her five years. Whether this activity intensifies, plateaus, or subsides by year 5 is not yet decided.

## Political Landscape

### The Arctic reshuffle
Because gate *quantity* (not quality — see the metaphysics document's locked distinction) is highest near the beacon's point of origin (the Arctic) and scales with territorial area, the Arctic Circle becomes the single most contested and gate-dense region on Earth almost overnight — a total inversion of its prior status as sparsely populated, low-power-projection territory. This doesn't invent new global players from nothing; it **elevates existing Arctic-holding nations** (the US via Alaska, Russia, Canada, and the Nordic states) into far greater relative significance than their prior GDP/military rankings alone would suggest, while nations with no Arctic claim at all lose relative ground regardless of their prior standing. **Crucially, this advantage is in volume, not fortune** — Arctic nations get more gates, not necessarily better ones; a resource-poor nation far from the Arctic could still get exceptionally lucky with a single high-value "contact" gate, while an Arctic nation's larger gate count could skew disproportionately hostile or monster-producing through sheer bad luck. Real, pre-existing Arctic sovereignty disputes (already a live issue in the real world) become sharply more consequential once the territory in question is also the most gate-dense on the planet, regardless of how that density ultimately pans out.

### The wartime coalition
No broad UN-style body ever achieves unified global authority (see the failed Year 1 summit, above) — but the Year 5 outbreak of open war against the hostile faction produces something narrower and more urgent: a reactive, NATO-adjacent mutual-defense coalition anchored specifically by the nations most exposed to the Arctic gate concentration and the frontier war zone. This is the live political stage Evelyn wakes into — an active coalition navigating an active war, not a settled postwar order.

### The US takeover of Stream
The United States — holding Arctic territory via Alaska, and therefore facing direct, immediate self-interest rather than abstract global desperation — invokes wartime emergency powers to take full operational control of every Stream Holding branch except I.O. (see Stream Holding Phase 3, above). This is not merely pressure or a seizure *attempt* — it succeeds completely at the operational level, while legal ownership of Evelyn's stake stays formally unresolved underneath it. Critically, control does not equal capability: Kusanagi's true hidden-tier arsenal, and likely other subsidiaries' Evelyn-gated ceilings, remain permanently inaccessible to anyone but her — the US gains ownership and deterrent symbolism, not the actual strategic leap that would have redrawn the balance of power.

### Open tension — the locked MAD equilibrium
Russia's substantial Arctic claims sit adjacent to, and in real-world tension with, NATO-aligned Arctic states (the Nordic countries, Canada, the US) — the crisis does not resolve this tension, and may sharpen it, since the same territory now carries far higher stakes than before, and the US takeover of Stream adds a new, unverifiable variable neither Russia nor China can price accurately. **Locked: this produces a genuine mutual-assured-destruction-style stalemate.** No Tier 1 power can confirm how much of Evelyn's true capability the US actually controls, and the uncertainty itself functions as the deterrent — everyone holds the theoretical potential to devastate everyone else, and precisely because no one can verify the real ceiling, no one moves. The world sits in a locked, uneasy distribution of power: catastrophic capability everywhere, active use nowhere.

### The power map — locked ranking

**Tier 1 — The reshuffled top.** Raw power, held uneasily, in a three-way standoff with echoes of a Cold War never fully thawed.
- **United States** — pre-crisis superpower compounded further: Arctic gate exposure via Alaska, home to Stream's primary hub, leads the Kusanagi seizure push, strong containment capacity from existing wealth. Old #1 stays #1, for a new mix of reasons.
- **Russia** — the single largest reversal on the board. Weaker economically pre-crisis, but its enormous Arctic land claim gives it the highest raw gate *quantity* on Earth, forcing every other power to take it far more seriously regardless of what that quantity actually yields.
- **China** — retains top-tier status on economic, military, and containment-capacity weight despite limited Arctic exposure; sheer territorial size still nets a meaningful gate count even at lower density.
- Relations among the three run cold and mutually distrustful by default — none can move against a Tier 2 gate-holder (below) without risking the other two gaining ground, which is the main thing keeping Tier 2 nations from being pressured into exclusive deals.

**Tier 2 — Elevated, and holding a resource Tier 1 cannot take by force.**
- **Canada** — the clearest "reshuffled" case: a pre-crisis middle power whose enormous Arctic territory now rivals much larger nations in gate count.
- **Nordic states**, anchored by **Norway** — very high gate density, capped by small land area, but outsized coalition leverage.
- **The EU-bloc**, anchored by **Germany** — old economic weight plus Arctic/Nordic member states gives it real standing even without matching any Tier 1 power individually.

**The three "golden gates" — locked.** Among all gates worldwide (recall: destination is genuinely random, independent of geography — see the metaphysics document), exactly three ultra-rare gates connect to civilizations both highly advanced and explicitly non-hostile. All three fall within Tier 2 nations, not Tier 1:
- **Canada** — one golden gate, as part of its broader Arctic gate volume.
- **Norway** (anchoring the Nordic bloc) — the second.
- **Germany** (anchoring the EU-bloc) — the third.

This is a deliberate inversion, not a coincidence of geography: the single most valuable resource on Earth — sustained access to an advanced, benevolent civilization — sits with nations too small to be threatening but too well-positioned to be ignored. The US, China, and Russia cannot simply seize access from any of the three, because each fears the other two gaining a foothold more than it wants to alienate the holder — the same mutual paranoia that keeps their own rivalry cold rather than hot now doubles as the Tier 2 holders' actual protection. It also creates rare, narrow moments of genuine Tier 1 cooperation — the three giants are far more likely to align with each other against a Tier 2 holder than to trust one another broadly, giving their otherwise-frozen relationship the occasional forced thaw.

**Tier 3 — Relevant through pre-existing weight, gate quantity a minor factor either way.**
- **India, UK, Japan, South Korea, France** — hold relevance mostly through pre-existing economic/military/tech weight and strong containment capacity.
- **Australia, Brazil** — large territory nets a moderate gate count purely from size, despite distance from the Arctic source.

## The Economy — How Money Works Now

### Flow, under new management
Flow was already deeply embedded in global banking, credit, and sovereign-level finance before the beacon event — systemically important enough that its stability matters to more than just Stream. Under the US takeover (see Phase 3, above), Flow's day-to-day infrastructure keeps running largely uninterrupted — the US government's overriding priority is continuity, since a faltering Flow risks a genuine global banking crisis nobody can contain. **Ownership changed hands; the plumbing didn't.** This is consistent with the broader "control without capability" pattern — the US now owns Flow, but has no more access to Evelyn's original design intentions or any hidden financial-side capability than it does with Kusanagi. Flow functions, but does not meaningfully advance, for the same structural reason nothing else under the takeover does.

### New asset classes
- **Gate real estate** — proximity to a "hot" (monster-producing) gate collapses land value; proximity to a "contact" or resource-rich gate creates instant boomtowns. A genuinely new, extremely volatile category of property risk with no actuarial precedent.
- **Gate access rights** — a new sovereign/private asset class analogous to mineral or drilling rights: the right to explore or extract from whatever lies beyond a specific gate, tradeable and disputable like any other resource claim.
- **Monster biomass economy** — organs, compounds, and materials harvested from culled monsters, feeding real commodity markets and drawing serious Helix-adjacent biotech interest (already established).
- **Enhanced labor market** — Tier-based compensation scales for military, security, and entertainment contracts; an entirely new insurance category built on no historical precedent, since underwriting the risk of employing or standing near a high-Tier individual has never existed before.

### Golden-gate trade — locked, a genuinely new alien economy
Real trade begins flowing through the three golden gates (Canada, Norway/Nordic bloc, Germany/EU-bloc) during these five years — not mere diplomatic contact, but actual goods, materials, and plausibly currency or currency-equivalents crossing over from genuinely advanced, non-hostile civilizations. This is the single strangest and most valuable economic development of the era: three middle-tier nations each hold exclusive access to an entirely new trading partner outside Earth's own economy, with no other nation able to replicate or bypass it. This sharply reinforces the Tier 2 "resource Tier 1 cannot take by force" dynamic already locked in the Political Landscape section — these three nations aren't just protected by Tier 1 mutual paranoia, they now hold a genuinely unique, ongoing revenue stream none of the giants can match.

### Energy Stream — a missed opportunity, deliberately
Despite the Earth's Wound (ambient zero-point leakage manifesting as geothermal and charged-particle anomalies — see above) sitting squarely in its wheelhouse as the one subsidiary with no hidden ceiling, **Energy Stream remains as paralyzed as every other branch during the five years.** This is not because the opportunity isn't real — it's that the takeover produced control without capability across the board, and Energy Stream is no exception: new leadership lacks Evelyn's expertise and doctrine-level judgment to move on anything meaningfully new, same as everywhere else. This leaves a genuine, valuable, unexploited resource sitting in plain sight — a strong candidate for either a story beat where an *outside* actor (a rival nation, a private venture, a desperate startup) moves first where Stream couldn't, or for Evelyn herself to seize once she returns, as one of the clearest, most concrete ways to demonstrate she's rebuilding from a position others already had five years to claim and didn't.
