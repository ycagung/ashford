# Evelyn Ashford — Armory

*Living document. Last updated: 2026-07-16*

---

## Overview

Two physically separate systems, split by what they're for.

- **Ground-Based Armory (Floor 4 of the underground lab):** Absolute Evelyn-only access, same tier as the command center (floor 3). Holds everything that's **combat-exclusive and ready-to-use** — no prototypes, no unfinished work — the same distinction that separates this floor from floor 6 (storage), which has no such requirement beyond fitting the space.
- **Orbital Storage:** A separate, unmanned orbital platform holding the handful of items that are genuinely exotic, one-of-a-kind, and Evelyn-exclusive — Getsurei, A1, and the suit. Deliberately kept as a **single unit**: more than one would defeat the rarity of what it holds and add failure points for no real benefit. Physically removed from the property entirely, consistent with the residence's own design brief — a presidential-level defense system that goes completely unnoticed from outside, with zero visible launch infrastructure anywhere on-site.
- **Who the ground floor is actually for:** almost none of it is built for Evelyn herself. Her body already out-performs most of what a vest, sidearm, or rifle offers — these exist for scenarios where *someone else* needs arming: an ally, an android, in circumstances severe enough to justify it. Those circumstances are catastrophic-level by default.
- **Who Orbital Storage is for:** Evelyn, exclusively, and only in situations severe enough to call for it. Getsurei and the suit both ride inside A1 as cargo rather than having independent delivery systems — a deliberate choice, not a cost-saving one: it ties both items to the same escalation threshold A1 itself answers to, so neither one becomes something she can reach for casually.

*For others; Evelyn would never need this herself.*

- Ballistic vests, plates, helmets, shields — standard protective-gear categories, but built entirely from Zylon/aramid-class composite rather than traditional solid-metal plating.
- Lighter and stronger than conventional equivalents, consistent with the material logic already running through her own body (tendon reinforcement, vascular structure) and Kusanagi's broader design philosophy.
- Scaled across sizes — human and android.

### Tier 2 — Firearms

- **Handguns:** Mostly Kusanagi-produced, predominantly chambered in 9mm, with some models in other calibers and a few revolvers. A handful of her personal favorites (Glock-adjacent commercial platforms) sit alongside the Kusanagi line rather than being replaced by it — she has a genuine preference for them.
- **Close-range:** Kusanagi tactical shotguns, a few models.
- **Rifles:** Two distinct threads —
  - Traditional M4-adjacent platforms, her personal preference, valued for modularity.
  - Kusanagi's flagship bullpup line, chambered in various calibers, built for precision and firepower over familiarity.
- **Ammunition:** No new calibers invented by Kusanagi or Evelyn — standard calibers throughout, but Kusanagi-manufactured rounds are modified for more output power and cleaner-burning propellant (less fouling/residue) versus commercial equivalents.
- **Kusanagi Flagship Suppressor:** Paired with the bullpup .50 cal precision rifle.
  - **Muzzle report suppression:** 60–70dB minimum reduction via proprietary multi-chamber/gas-dispersion design — far beyond any real-world suppressor, but scoped honestly to the muzzle blast only.
  - **Explicitly not claiming:** suppression of the supersonic crack generated by the round itself in flight — physically unsolvable, left alone rather than hand-waved. At .50 cal supersonic velocity, the round arrives at target well ahead of any sound cue (muzzle report or crack), so in practice the target is rarely in a position to "hear it coming" regardless.
  - **Durability:** ~300x a standard suppressor's service life. Solves the real failure modes of conventional cans — baffle erosion/melting from repeated hot-gas cutting, and mounting fatigue/loosening under sustained fire — via a Zylon-class composite baffle stack resistant to that erosion.

## Part Two — Orbital Storage

### Platform

- **Location:** Unmanned orbital satellite, **~800 km altitude**, **near-polar (~90° inclination) low Earth orbit**.
- **Orbital period:** ~100.7 minutes — the platform sweeps over every latitude on Earth, poles included, as the planet rotates beneath it over the course of a day.
- **Why 800 km:** Atmospheric drag at this altitude is roughly 2–3 orders of magnitude lower than at typical crewed-station altitudes (~400 km) — the same altitude band real decommissioned satellites are parked at for centuries-scale orbital lifetimes. This solves orbital decay *for free*, without any station-keeping thrust — a genuine "no failure points" result, not an engineered-around one. It also sits comfortably below the inner Van Allen radiation belt (~1,000 km+), avoiding a radiation-shielding problem for a sensitive, irreplaceable payload.
- **Why polar inclination:** Chosen over a Molniya-type high-latitude-dwell orbit specifically because Evelyn has no way to predict where a future threat originates — true global coverage was prioritized over Arctic-weighted dwell time, matching the "most versatile option" the platform was built around.
- **Why a single unit:** Deliberate. A second platform would defeat the rarity of what it carries and add a failure point without a real operational benefit.
- **Defense/concealment of the platform itself:** Not yet discussed — open question below.

### Getsurei ("Soul of the Moon")

*Relocated here from the ground-floor armory. Spec unchanged from original build — reproduced here for completeness.*

- **Appearance:** Deliberately unassuming. Black blade, black sheath, no ornamentation.
- **Construction:** Forged and synthesized — high-tensile improved-tamahagane steel combined with a bespoke composite material, fusing ultimate sharpness and durability together, with vibration absorption (damping, not flex) despite fully rigid construction. Consistent with real differential-hardening katana metallurgy taken to a fictional tier.
- **Locked feat:** at full committed force, Evelyn can cleanly slash through a solid reinforced concrete pillar up to ~41cm square (~0.168 m² cross-section) in a single swing — cross-referenced from the Physical Specifications document's Cutting Test.
- **Carry logic:** Not worn day-to-day. Now rides inside A1 as stowed cargo rather than sitting armory-resident on the ground floor — delivered only when A1 itself is dispatched.
- **Narrative role:** A deliberate "final form, full power" story beat. Tying its delivery to A1's own deployment threshold reinforces this rather than undercutting it — Getsurei is never reachable on its own, only ever alongside the same escalation that calls A1 in.

### A1 — Companion Unit

**Core functions:**
1. **Material storage** — physically carries the suit's particle-based material reserve and Getsurei as cargo. This is the mechanism that solves the "where did that much material come from" problem for the suit: the mass already exists inside A1; deploying the suit onto Evelyn's body is a transfer/reconfiguration, never a creation.
2. **Donning rig** — A1 physically morphs so the suit opens in all its parts and Evelyn steps inside; not instant, and A1 is genuinely vulnerable during the process (she has to create a distraction). A1 is never consumed into becoming the suit — it releases the material once donning completes and continues operating independently (functions 3–5 below keep working whether or not she's currently suited up).
3. **Backup computational unit** — local compute redundancy, consistent with AEGIS's own established doctrine of not being a single-point, all-seeing system.
4. **AEGIS remote interface** — gives AEGIS a physical presence at the scene (hands-on telemetry, real-time combat support) without drawing on the neural-link bandwidth that already competes with Evelyn's own situational awareness (Physical Specs, Section 7).
5. **Autonomous positioning** — hovers and moves at a safe distance intelligently, maintaining itself even without an active AEGIS connection.
6. **The Dome launcher** — carries a separate, dedicated mass of radiation-shielding material (not drawn from the suit's own particle pool) and launches it to synthesize around Evelyn on demand, stacking with Shell-mode protection. Carrying this dedicated mass is why A1 scales up in size — plausibly as large as the original "Veronica" reference point — costing thrust-to-weight for Orb/Needle but directly improving shielding capacity (more mass = more protection, no clever trick needed).
7. **Self-defense** — two independent layers, deliberately bounded rather than an absolute "repels everything" claim:
   - **Layer 1 — plasma-based deflection field.** Ionizes the air into a dense plasma layer that deposits energy into incoming projectiles rather than exerting a mystery repulsive force on them — real, if early-stage, physics (laser-induced plasma shielding research). Effective against kinetic/ballistic threats (bullets, shrapnel); ineffective against large/slow objects or energy-based threats. Reflexive, threshold-activated against sudden high-force impact rather than an always-on wall, and bounded by A1's own power reserve rather than free.
   - **Layer 2 — three-layer physical housing.** Outer: shear-stiffening composite shell, reusing the same dilatant-material principle already locked for Evelyn's own epidermis (soft under normal handling, instantly stiffening under sudden impact). Mid: Zylon-class composite weave for tensile strength and crack-arrest. Inner: dense structural metal plate for raw compressive/shear absorption — with a genuine free bonus, a solid metal inner layer functions as a Faraday cage, giving A1 real EMP hardening for its own electronics.
- **Power source:** Independent, bounded onboard reserve (battery/capacitor-class storage) — not an unlimited remote draw on Evelyn's Zero Core. Topped up opportunistically via Basilisk mode (below). This makes A1 operating far from Evelyn for extended periods a genuine resource constraint, not a non-issue — consistent with the rest of this build's discipline of real, honest limits.

**Three modes — one continuous reconfiguring material, three postures, named by shape:**

- **Orb** — VTOL/hover form. The baseline standby posture: hovering at a safe distance, providing defensive and combat support, intelligent autonomous positioning.
- **Needle** — hypersonic transit form. Minimal cross-section for drag/heat reduction, with just enough retractable control surfaces (swept flush in cruise, deployed for the glide phases) to generate the lift a true needle shape can't — a deliberate, acknowledged compromise against pure aerodynamic purity, needed to execute a boost-glide profile.
- **Basilisk** — ground mode. Not legged — a point-first form driven directly into the ground, the same elongated nose-first geometry as Needle, just reoriented into what it's piercing. Functions: a physical transponder/relay (a literal ground-contact element improves signal coupling, extending AEGIS's reach into signal-denied environments like the current Arctic scenario) and an energy harvester (geothermal/seismic/vibration energy tapped directly through the point of contact, replenishing A1's bounded power reserve).

**Deployment profile — immediate launch, no repositioning wait:**

Rather than waiting for Orbital Storage to pass near Evelyn's position (which, at 800km/~100.7min period, would mean up to ~50 minutes for a half-orbit transit plus ~25–30 minutes of descent in the worst case — roughly 75–80 minutes total), A1 launches immediately from wherever Orbital Storage currently sits in its orbit, using the orbital velocity it already carries (~7.45 km/s at 800km) as stored lateral kinetic energy rather than idling.

- **Needle-mode hybrid profile:** a shallow boost-glide phase (skip/glide using stored orbital energy, same underlying principle as the Space Shuttle's reentry or modern hypersonic glide vehicles) covers the first few thousand km, transitioning into powered airbreathing hypersonic cruise for the remaining distance in a true worst-case (antipodal) deployment.
- **Total worst-case transit:** roughly **40–55 minutes**, faster than the repositioning-then-descend approach above, though still somewhat slower than a hypothetical self-launched ground-based suborbital system (~25–35 minutes) — a trade deliberately accepted in exchange for zero visible launch infrastructure anywhere on Evelyn's property.
- **Thermal note:** the shallow extended glide trades a lower peak heat spike for a longer total/sustained heat load versus a steep reentry profile — Needle's armor/heat handling needs to be understood against sustained thermal stress specifically, not just a brief spike.

### The Suit

*Relocated here; stored as cargo inside A1 rather than as a static item in the ground armory. A1 is the dedicated carrier/donning rig for the suit — A1 itself never becomes the suit (see A1 section below).*

#### Particle mechanism

- **Scale — mesoscale, not true nanoscale.** True nanometer-scale "nanobots" run into real, unsolved physics problems (van der Waals stiction dominating over inertia, no room for per-unit power/computation at that density). The honest version is sub-millimeter-to-millimeter mesoscale units, modeled on real **claytronics/catom** research (Carnegie Mellon and others) — individually simple, dumb units that bond/debond with neighbors to collectively form shapes. Not invented physics; an extension of real, actively-researched engineering.
- **Each unit is individually dumb.** No meaningful onboard intelligence or propulsion per unit — there's no room for it at this scale, same honest reasoning as the nanoscale rejection above. All actual coordination/"thinking" lives in centralized compute (AEGIS/A1), not in the matter itself.
- **Consequence — no open-air swarming.** A particle can't fly independently to a destination; it only moves by staying mechanically connected to its neighbors and reconfiguring against them. This means the suit cannot self-assemble from a shapeless stored mass — it's stored **pre-formed into major parts** (torso, helmet, pants, shoes), with "shapeshifting" limited to joint articulation and fine-fit adjustment, not macro-formation from scratch. Visually and tactilely, this makes the suit read as **fully mechanical** — visible segment lines, plates, joints — not a smooth liquid-metal skin.
- **Individual unit material:** Zylon-class composite shell per unit, consistent with the material family used everywhere else in this build.

#### Bonding — two separate systems, not one

The suit's structural integrity and its reconfiguration ability are deliberately **not** the same mechanism — this is what resolves the EMP vulnerability described below.

- **Active layer (reconfiguration):** electromagnetic/electrostatic bonding — units making and breaking connections in a coordinated, massively parallel pattern to shift shape (the same "wave through a crowd" principle as real claytronics — individual units don't travel far, the pattern of connection does). Requires continuous power and live compute. This is the layer that's genuinely EMP-vulnerable.
- **Passive layer (structural holding):** a **mechanical interlock** — physical, geometric keying between units (dovetail/hook-and-catch/ratchet-style), not an active field. **Working analogy: e-ink.** Like an e-ink pixel, each connection is bistable — it physically latches into a state and holds it with zero ongoing power, only drawing current during the moment it changes. This is a real, standard fail-safe engineering principle (the same logic as aircraft landing-gear mechanical downlocks) — losing power means "stays locked in place," not "falls apart."
- **Locking is deliberately costly, not free:** once set, the mechanical interlock stays locked until actively released — reconfiguring again requires first unlocking, not an instant toggle. This keeps a real time/energy cost attached to every shape change, consistent with everything else in this build.

#### EMP behavior

- **What fails:** the active layer only — she loses the ability to reconfigure/shapeshift. The suit does not disintegrate, because the passive layer was never drawing power to hold its current shape.
- **What survives:** if the passive layer's structure includes dense/conductive material (as it does, see configurations below), that shielding keeps protecting the Zero Core and network interface underneath even while the suit's active layer is "offline" — the same incidental-Faraday-cage logic already established for A1's own inner metal layer.
- **"Reboot":** EMP strike leaves the suit frozen in whatever configuration it was in at the moment of the hit — not catastrophic failure, a bounded, specific loss (adaptability down, protection intact) consistent with this build's "honest limit, not a clean fix" discipline. Recovery = AEGIS/A1's link re-establishing and re-syncing control with the suit's active layer; exact duration not yet pinned down.
- **Deliberate design choice — no visible state-tell.** Unlike Zero Mode's blue glow or the AR eye's emissive quality, the suit stays fully, deliberately black with no indicator of mode or status. A departure from the rest of this build's "make real state changes visible" doctrine, chosen on purpose — same "unassuming" instinct behind Getsurei's plain black sheath, applied here instead.

#### Donning — via A1, not self-transformation

- **A1 is the donning rig.** Rather than the suit assembling itself around her (too slow, too anime-transformation-sequence for a real combat scenario), **A1 physically morphs so the suit opens in all its parts and she steps/goes inside** — deployment is a real, not-instant physical act.
- **A1 is vulnerable during this process** — she has to actively create a distraction for the brief window donning takes. A real tactical cost, not a free equip.
- **A1 remains independent afterward (locked: Option 3).** A1 is not consumed or absorbed into becoming the suit — once donning is complete, A1 releases the suit material and continues operating as a separate, lighter, still-autonomous unit (its own flight, defense, and AEGIS-interface functions keep functioning independently of whether she's currently suited up).
- **Regular clothing cannot be worn under the suit.** The suit's seals and mechanical interlocks require direct, consistent contact with her body — real-world precedent: astronauts never suit up over street clothes either, only a thin form-fitting liner. As part of the donning sequence, **A1 shreds whatever she's currently wearing.**
- **The one exception is a purpose-built base wear** — a thin layer using the same Zylon-class composite family as everything else in this build. A1 doesn't *recognize* or spare it; it survives because it's built tough enough to withstand the same shredding force applied to everything else, while ordinary clothing isn't. No detection system required, and nothing to fail or be spoofed mid-sequence.
- **The base wear is comfort/modesty only — not a functional requirement.** She doesn't wear it at all times (it wouldn't make sense to), so going into the suit without it is a real possibility, not an edge case. The suit's passive layer is already fully capable of holding its own structural shape and managing the interface against bare skin — **it actually functions slightly better without an intermediate layer**, not worse. Skipping the base wear carries zero tactical cost, purely a personal/character choice.

#### Resupply and repair

- **Self-repair is reconfiguration only** — reflowing *existing* material to fix damage, never regrowth of material that's actually been destroyed or severed away. Catastrophic damage to the suit stays catastrophic.
- **Replacing genuinely lost material requires real, physical contact with A1.** This is distinct from reconfiguring among material she already has (below) — "redistributing what I own" and "I'm short material and need more" are not the same cost.

#### Mode-switching — one finite pool, genuinely vulnerable mid-change

- **Base, Vacuum, and Shell draw from one shared, finite particle pool** — not three separate material sets. The suit cannot exist in two configurations at once; switching is a real reconfiguration event using the same active/passive bonding mechanism as initial donning, just rearranging material already on her body rather than assembling from scratch.
- **She is genuinely vulnerable during a switch** — not a masked transition. Because it's the same material being redistributed rather than two states coexisting, there is no way for the old configuration to stay locked and protective while the new one comes online. She has to actively choose the moment to switch, the same category of tactical decision as the donning distraction.
- **A back-mounted reserve unit** (visually: a circular bump at the back, Tron-Legacy-adjacent) houses two independent reserves, solving two different problems:
  1. **Extra particle material** for Vacuum/Shell configurations — lets her reconfigure among Base/Vacuum/Shell **without needing direct A1 contact**, since the material is already on her person. (Contact with A1 remains required only for replacing material that's been destroyed/lost outright, not for mode-switching.)
  2. **Reserve coolant**, beyond what's normally carried in her own body — directly answers the sustained-extreme-heat vulnerability (her ~44.5kW cooling-capacity ceiling), the same way the material reserve answers mode-switching.
  - This also clarifies *why* Base is genuinely the lightest, least complex configuration: it's the minimum-material state the pool can be in, not a separate system — "less to break" is a direct, honest consequence of using less of the pool at once.

#### Configurations

**1. Base**
- Deep black, mechanically flexible, minimal material draw from the pool — "doesn't do much," which is the point: fewer systems means fewer failure points, and full unrestricted movement.
- Solves: cut/puncture resistance (an open gap left in the Physical Specs skin build), the punch-through contradiction (gauntlet-level reinforcement at the striking surfaces), and Zero Core targeting.
- **Zero Core protection via camouflage, not conspicuous armor:** the suit runs thicker across her chest, which doubles as visual cover for the actual Zero Core shielding underneath (the Core sits physically between her breasts, per its already-locked placement) — a deliberate choice to avoid "heavily armored specific zone" reading as an obvious tell to a smart enemy. Explicitly the same design philosophy already running through Getsurei's unassuming appearance and the coolant bladder's no-external-tell construction — now established as a repeated, intentional doctrine across multiple independent systems, not a one-off trick.

**2. Vacuum**
- Fully sealed, additional layers over Base — the seal costs real joint articulation (reduced degrees of freedom, less acrobatic), though still functional for striking/dodging.
- Solves: vacuum/space survival (addressing the real ebullism risk flagged below, not just "no oxygen"), deep-sea pressure, EMP (Faraday shielding via the passive layer's conductive mass), a partial contribution to radiation (via the same added mass), and chemical/biological exposure (total physical seal — the only real option given her pain receptors and nervous system are permanently, deliberately unmodified with no biological countermeasure available).
- **Honest limit on the chem/bio seal:** it's only as good as its integrity — meaning the exact moment of a reconfiguration/donning vulnerability window is also the moment of greatest exposure to this threat. One mechanic, multiple genuine failure modes.

**3. Shell**
- Considerably thicker and bulkier than Vacuum — reads visually like a "strength mode" trope, but isn't one; its actual job is radiation.
- Solves: a heavy dose of radiation protection, via real layered mass shielding (not a clever field) — dense high-Z material (lead/tungsten-class) for gamma/X-ray absorption, paired with a hydrogen-rich/boron-doped moderator layer for neutrons, since the two radiation types need genuinely different countermeasures.
- **Honest limit, consistent with the skin's own already-locked ceiling:** this extends her partial radiation protection further, but doesn't erase it — full immunity at detonation/neutron tier stays off the table, since real shielding at that level requires genuinely impractical mass.
- **Can call in "The Dome"** — see below.

**The Dome**
- A separate, dedicated mass of shielding material — **not** drawn from the suit's own particle pool, explicitly to avoid a three-way resource conflict between Base/Vacuum/Shell and the Dome.
- Carried and **launched** by A1 (not requiring A1 to physically dock/arrive the way full donning does — A1 only needs to be within effective launch range, exact range not yet defined).
- Synthesizes around her as a large mass of additional shielding on demand, stacking on top of Shell mode's own protection for the heaviest radiation scenarios.
- **A1 sizing consequence:** carrying the Dome's dedicated material means A1 itself scales up in size/mass (plausibly as large as the original "Veronica" reference point) — this doesn't cost orbital transit speed (orbital velocity depends on altitude, not mass) but does cost thrust-to-weight for VTOL/Orb hover and Needle's powered-cruise phase, meaning a bulkier A1 needs proportionally more absolute thrust for the same responsiveness. The upside is direct and favorable: more mass is quite literally more shielding, the same "no clever trick, just more material" honesty already established for radiation countermeasures generally.
