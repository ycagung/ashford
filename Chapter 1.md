Have you had an experience of complete drowning? No? I suppose not. If you do, most likely you're not in any living condition to tell the story. I do, I am currently drowning in this cold, pitch-black unknown water without even knowing which way is up or down. I don't remember much too. The closest thing I remember is punching Kenway's missile containing a cluster of smaller neutron bombs. Ah, I remember the searing pain from my chest. Like it was torn apart both slowly and violently. Never have I experienced something like that in my life. That's coming from someone who injected a body altering serum to her spinal cord while fully awake. Then after that searing pain... Nothing. Pitch black. The next thing I know is that I'm drowning like this.

Why am I not dead, you ask? Because I already am, a long time ago. Well, to be precise, the traditional state of "living" applied to normal human being doesn't apply to me. I live in a bit different condition, and will be dead under different ones too. Drowning, for instance, won't kill me.

*Danger! Low coolant reserve, resupply immediately!*

*Muscle recovery success!*

*Coolant resupply success!*

*Outer shell regeneration success!*

*Warning! Communication systems are offline!*

*Warning! Navigation system needs immediate calibration!*

My eyes are suddenly lighting up with a bunch of notifications. Good thing. It means my systems are working. Now I just have to resurface.

Channeling some energy to my right hand, I look at it as it heats up the water around it. Bubbles starts to form around it and immediately goes to the direction where "up" is. 

*Visual system calibration success!*

Finally, good timing. My normal vision changes to infrared one. Supposedly this one will enable me to see in this pitch black. But no, nothing, still pitch black. How deep am I?

No choice, I flail my arms and legs around and start to swim upwards. Up until the wall of ice stops me.
