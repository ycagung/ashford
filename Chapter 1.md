# Chapter 1 - Cold Start

---

Have you had an experience of complete drowning? No? I suppose not. If you do, most likely you're not in any living condition to tell the story. I do, I am currently drowning in this cold, pitch-black unknown water without even knowing which way is up or down. I don't remember much either. The closest thing I remember is punching Kenway's missile containing a cluster of smaller neutron bombs. Ah, I remember the searing pain from my chest. Like it was torn apart both slowly and violently. Never have I experienced something like that in my life. That's coming from someone who injected a body altering serum to her spinal cord while fully awake. Then after that searing pain... Nothing. Pitch black. The next thing I know is that I'm drowning like this.

Why am I not dead, you ask? Because I already am, a long time ago. Well, to be precise, the traditional state of "living" applied to normal human being doesn't apply to me. I live in a bit different condition, and will be dead under different ones too. Drowning, for instance, won't kill me.

*Danger! Low coolant reserve, resupply immediately!*

*Muscle recovery success!*

*Coolant resupply success!*

*Outer shell regeneration success!*

*Alert! Communication systems are offline!*

*Alert! Navigation system needs immediate calibration!*

*Alert! Cannot reach global time network!*

My eyes are suddenly lighting up with a bunch of notifications. Good thing. It means my systems are working. Now I just have to resurface.

Channeling some energy to my right hand, I look at it as it heats up the water around it. Bubbles starts to form around it and immediately goes to the direction where "up" is. 

*Visual system calibration success! Activating infrared vision.*

Finally, good timing. Now I'll be able to see some... Uh... Still nothing. How deep am I? Why didn't AEGIS do something about this? Fine, can't do anything about it. I flail my arms and legs around, propelling myself upwards. Up until the wall of ice stops me. THUD! Ouch! Somebody ought to put a sign on this wall. 

Feeling the ice surface, I can't seem to find any crack or opening to exploit. I need to create my own. 

"HAH!"

With one strong and fast movement, I send my knuckles onto the ice wall. Then again. And again. Until an audible *'crack!'* can be heard. Three punches, I think it's doable. I punch again right into the crack, and again, again, again. Even when I can taste the iron from my bloody hand, I don't stop. I keep on punching my way out.

When there's enough of an opening, the punching turns into digging. By digging I mean grabbing a block of ice and tearing it away. If there's nothing to grab, I just punch it again until it cracks. 

Minutes gone, I'm making progress. At least I think I did. But I don't care, sooner or later, I'll be out of this frozen prison. Then hours gone. I still don't stop.

When I think I have spent a day digging and clawing my way out of the ice, the light comes. As blurry as the image from the surface looks like, a finish line still sends a bit of relief to its bloodied runner. I stop, leaning my back to the jagged wall of ice behind me. I don't experience tiredness in my state now, I'm not capable of it. But I can still feel pain. My bloodied hands hang while my auto-regeneration kicks in. If I'm breathing right now, I'd breathe a big relief.

One last punch. That's all it will take. 

---

The ice burst open with steam as I leap out of it. When I finally hit the ground, my knees immediately give out. I cough and vomit violently as my body tries to push out all the water in my lung. 

*Respiratory system recovery success!*

Finally. Now I can lay back and wait until my comms are rebooted.

*Rebooting communication system. Please wait.*

I can feel the cold ice directly on my back skin. Do I not wear anything? Ah, right, my clothes were torn when I push dilation mode to the max. I look under and I can see that I, thankfully, still wearing a sports bra and my pants are there, well, mostly. I'd kill for some warm clothes right now. 

Then a high-speed shadow passes through my vision. Whatever it is, it's just jumped directly above me. Quickly I get up and regain my footing. My eyes scanning left and right to find anything that moves. Found it!

The shadow is flanking me from the right! I jump and land an equally fast roundhouse kick, catching it right as it approaches me. It falls down headfirst onto the ice, creating a small crater and immediately bursts his head open from the impact. Only then I can get a good look of what it is. 

It's a wolf, at least it looks like one. Aside from the vibrant blue fur covering half of its white body in a pretty cool tribal pattern, another tail sprouting on its back, and what looks like static electricity arching around its body, yeah it's a wolf. Now I'm more curious who created this creature. There's no way it's a regular snow wolf.

"Hey! You! What are you doing here? Who are you?"

I turn over and see a man wearing hunting clothes. Well, if this is 1600s, that hunting clothes would be pretty trendy. Funnily enough, he carries an AR15, pointing at me. Pretty bizarre set of looks if I say so myself, but then, I'm the one standing at the arctic wearing undergarments. 

"Why don't you introduce yourself first? It's a common courtesy when you ask for someone's name."

He walks closer, still pointing his rifle at me, then he catches the view of the weird wolf beside me. 

"Is that... a lightning wolf? Did it attack you? Are you okay?" He quickly lowers his weapon and runs toward me. 

"Uh... A lightning wolf, you say? Pretty accurate name I suppose. Yeah, it attacked me earlier. I'm okay." He kneels to make sure the wolf is trully dead. "Don't worry, I bashed its head open. There's no way it's still alive."

He gets up and faces me again. "Is that why your clothes... Er..."

"No. This is caused by something else." He takes off his fur jacket and offers it to me. Such a gentleman. Even though I can immediately see him visibly shaking.

"The name's Kurt. Wear this. You must be freezing." 

"Nice to meet you Kurt, you can call me Eve. And as much as I love a true gentleman, I think you need it more than me. I'll be fine. I've been in this state for quite some time."

He doesn't immediately pull back the jacket. Instead, he stares at me oddly, tilting his head a bit.

"Are you an enhanced?"

Now I'm the one who looks at him oddly. Yes, I am enhanced. Though I think it's not what he means. Nobody knows I'm enhanced, and he's asking like 'enhanced person' is something common. 

"What are you talking about?"

"You know... Enhanced. Like the ones on the internet busting out monsters from the gates."

"Gates?"

ROOOAAAARRRRR!!!

A sudden loud roar coming from the west, cutting our conversation short. The next thing we know, a big bear is towering over us. One of its hands with glowing hot claws in a trajectory to slash us out of existence. Then, BAM! The ground breaks, spewing ice and steam everywhere. In an instant I was moving to shield Kurt and I managed to redirect its claws to the ground beside me instead. 

"Run!" I shout at Kurt from my shoulder.

The next attack is coming much faster that when I turn my head to face the bear, the claws are already centimeters in front of me. Fuck! No other choice.

"Dilate." 

The world feels like it tries to zoom out and slow down. But it failed. The claws already dig into my chest and sends me flying away until I hit an ice boulder about five meters away.

What's happening? I can't use my power? What's going on with my body?

"Eve!" I can hear Kurt's distant shout as he's running away from the bear. 

Urgh! I can see blood dripping from my chest as I try to stand up again. I need to finish this quickly, or Kurt will be killed. I clench my fist, flex my muscles and with all my strength push the ground. In an instant, my fist meets the bear's head and obliterate it. 

My foot skids on the ice trying to slow me down. When I finally stop, I can see the bears body falling to the ground with a loud THUD!

"You are an enhanced." Says Kurt as he too falls on his bottom, shaking from the adrenaline. I don't answer, instead I squat in front of the bear's headless corpse. 

This bear is weird too, it has four arms. Almost sends me laughing.

"What's this one's name?" I ask Kurt.

"White Grizzly." He says.

"Funny name. You know, I never caught why you're here at all. Isn't this area too remote for a person to wander by himself?"

"Well, I can say the same for you. I'm what people call a solo hunter. I'm also an enhanced, just a low-level one."

I tilt my head. "Solo hunter?"

"Have you been living under a rock?"

"Ice actually. But carry on."

"Hunter is a person who hunts the monster that comes out of a gate. And this area is pretty packed with them."

"Really? And why would you endanger yourself to hunt some monsters if you know you're not that strong?"

"Money. We sell the corpses afterwards."

*Communication system has been rebooted!* The message comes along with some static noise.

*"E... Eve... Eve, can you hear me. It's AEGIS. Please say something."*

"Excuse me for a moment. Need to take this." I gesture at my ear while saying that to Kurt, then leave him with a big question mark above his head. "AEGIS. Talk to me."

*"Oh, my... It's really you! I'm so glad. I thought you were dead!"*

"Well, you can say that I dodged this one. Though I'm thoroughly confused about a few things, AEGIS. What's with all these... Creatures? I killed two already. A strange electric wolf and a bear with four arms."

*"Eve..."*

"What makes me even more confused is how is it possible that all these things came to existence overnight?"

*"Eve... It has been five years."*

"WHAT?!"
