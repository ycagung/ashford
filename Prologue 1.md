# Prologue 1 - Everything, Everywhere, All at Once

---

"Yes, the man in blue," I said, pointing my finger to the journalist at the back of the crowd. While a thousand camera flashes are hitting me relentlessly like a bunch of M50s operated by poor-sighted soldiers. I'd prefer the M50s, actually. Something that I can really dodge, or deflect for that matter. Whatever.

"Evelyn, you just hit ten million subs on YouTube the other day. How do you feel about that?" The man in blue speaks.

Really? Elementary level question? Does AI really lower journalist's quality that much nowadays? I should hold back on releasing a new one, then.

"You should ask YouTube how it feels instead. Next question, please." Well, that's the best I can do. Brush it off, at least some of the guys laugh at my answer. Because I really don't feel anything different. What am I even going to do with my audience anyway? I started uploading just because I need some outlet to let out some steam, it's not really that fun managing a multi-national, multi-entity, tech empire with a lot of secret of my own-making. Plus, Reflective guys said that it would be good for PR, and turns out it really does.

A man in a bomber jacket now raises up. "Miss Ashford," he said, this one is polite at least. "You are twenty-eight, the sole founder and currently the biggest stakeholder of the largest tech corporation in the world," he continues. I can see the nerve in his demeanor, slight tremble in his hands, looks like this is his first time questioning me or someone like me.

"Is there a question in that?" I decide to push it anyway, see if he can take the pressure.

"Yes. Why keep that layer of yours visible at all? The YouTuber slash celebrity-face layer."

Oh, look, a decent question. He's not bad for a rookie.

"First of all, I'm not a celebrity. Second, because it's fun. I love fun things. That will be all for today, thank you all."

With that, I leave the bright podium, followed by even more flashes of light than before. 

---

"The recon is finished?" I asked. My hands holding the steering wheel as I speed up on the highway. I have a very important schedule today. Far more important than the gala I just left earlier. 

*"Not yet. But it's fascinating how they built their compound in the middle of nowhere. Looks really like an evil compound from the movies."* A mix of robotic and organic voice speaks deep inside my head.

"Did you just enhance your voice matrix? You sound a lot more organic."

*"How sweet of you to notice. I've sent the most recent recon file to your interface."*

A bunch of folder icons shows up in my peripheral vision.

"Yep, later. I'm driving."

*Beep. Autopilot activated. Navigating to... home.* Then my car starts to steer on its own.

*"Now you can check the files."*

"You're no fun."

My hands move from the steering wheel and start gesturing like I'm operating an invisible screen. It is invisible, just not to me. The AR module in my eyes is now flooding me with a bunch of documents, maps, and 3D projections. 

They really did build their compound in the Arctic. Really strange. I can't see any benefit to it other than complete isolation from the world. 

"Let's see... Looks like three layer of high-density steel gates, air-locked in each one, about a hundred trained personnel by the looks of their movement, armed with military rifles. Four SAMs in each cardinal direction. Do you see anything else, AEGIS?"

*"The compound has multiple floors underground. I can see ten, maybe more, the recon satellite couldn't get too deep."*

"Where is the target?"

*"Fifth underground floor."*

"Right... It wouldn't be fun if it's too easy. Prepare the hover jet, I'll depart right away."

*"But, Eve. We still have to wait until we map all the floors. It will take some time. The recon sentry has to navigate deeper."*

"No. We don't have time. We know where they'll send the first payload. They may be preparing for it right this moment."

---

*"You should at least put on the armor."*

AEGIS's voice sounds too clear considering I'm standing in my hover jet with the door open. That's the benefit of direct-neural communication, I suppose. 

"Don't have time to dress up. They wouldn't mind, I'm sure. This is not a formal occasion."

*"It's not them I'm worried about. People don't usually sky dive from fifty thousand feet in a bomber jacket and sneakers. THEN carry an infiltration mission. You're not Tom Cruise."*

I check on my handgun once again and holster it back.

"Funny, he's my inspiration. Keep your eyes open, AEGIS."

With that, I let go of the wall and dive head first into Arctic's night sky.

---

My hand knocks on the steel door three times like a polite person.

"Excuse me. I'm looking for Mr. Kenway."

No answer. Alright, force it is. 

I throw a light punch and in an instant, the steel door deformed like a crumpled paper and was blown away, revealing what's inside. Or rather, who.

A man leaning on his desk, dressed like a formal military man. Quite a bit older than what I remembered. Yeah, well, my memory of him was from twenty years ago anyway.

"Good evening. You didn't answer your door, so I took the liberty of opening my own way. How are you doing, Mr. Kenway? You look rather... Old."

"Evelyn Ashford." He says with an impossibly straight face. It isn't a facade either, his heartbeat, micro-gestures, nothing on him says that he's even a bit scared. Even when a strange woman blew his door with her bare hands. "You think you can just barge into my compound, kill my men-"

"Whoa, I'm going to cut you off there. I didn't kill any of your men. They're just sleeping."

I point my thumb to the back, where a dozen men are laying on the ground unconscious.

*"Three are dead. Caught stray bullets from their friendlies."* AEGIS says in the comm.

*"That's not on me."* I reply voiceless.

"What? What are you talking about? You, alone, incapacitated hundreds of men without killing any?"

"Is that so hard to believe?"

He goes silent for a while. I can't tell if he's weighing something or just stalling.

"What do you want?" He finally breaks the silence.

"Finally, a relevant question. You see, when you said my name, I thought you'd remember who my parents were-"

"I remember. William and June Ashford. Is this a revenge, then?"

*Crack!*

The sound comes from behind him, where a bullet casing embedded deep in the wall. My hand flicked it instinctively when he mentioned my parents' name. His eyes are wide open, surprised either by the projectile or me. I reach down to pick up a small steel piece that was broken from the door I punched earlier. 

"Next time you cut me off, I won't aim for the wall. Do we have an agreement?"

He nods slowly.

"Now where were we? Ah, right. Is this a revenge? As much as I want to break your chest cavity and stitch it back again and again until you beg me for the gift of death... No. I'm better than that. The trauma of you shooting my parents in front of my eyes won't be enough to change who I am. Besides... If I wanted to, I'd have done it a long time ago."

He gulps. A bead of sweat runs down his forehead. Now I can see his heart rate starts to spike up. Looks like he realizes what I'm looking for. Let's see if he's prepared for it, I'll try to taunt him more.

"Come on. Now's when you ask me again."

"What do you really want, then?"

"You see. It's not enough that you killed my parents for NOTHING Because you weren't patient enough to wait for the government to destroy their life work. You are now developing the very weapon my parents were trying to dismantle. So tell me, where do you keep Project Nova?"

"Ha... Hahahaha. HAHAHAHAHA!" A loud laughter erupts from his mouth. 

"What's so funny, hm?"

"Here I thought you came for a petty revenge. Which I would give."

"Really? You don't value your own life?"

"No. Not above this project."

*"Eve-"* AEGIS is calling me.

"You see, Evelyn..." At the same time, Kenway is trying to say something.

*"The recon sentry just reported. We've found where he keeps the first payload."*

"I'd love to give you the location and your revenge, if you want any."

*"Where is it?"* "Where is it?" My voice and my mind ask the same question to different person. Somewhere deep inside me know that this will go wrong. Just judging by the sudden change in both their tones. AEGIS sounds like panicking, where Kenway... he sounds even calmer than before. Both of them feels to me like there will be no tomorrow.

*"It's down below, Eve. Far under you. And it's already-"*

"It's here, Evelyn. Under your nose." He takes out a trigger device from his pocket and point it at me. And. *Click!*

*"It's moving now! You have to hurry!"*

A violent rumble vibrates the entire structure. Walls cracking, lights flickering, floors shaking. 

Now I get it. He built his compound here is because he knew exactly what my satellites are capable of. And the only thing that will slow my reconnaissance power is just... depth. He placed Project Nova, a neutron bomb with a lower yield than the ones in the cold war but spreads its gamma radiation in a much much wider area. The last recon file calculated its coverage to be about the size of the state of Texas. They're trying to kill people without destroying much of anything else. 

"Let's go down together." 

I take a deep breath, close my eyes. In a matter of just two seconds the trembling world seems to forget its state. Replaced by a new state of stillness. The temperature drops violently, water droplets form around me, the desk, the walls, then instantly turn into ice particles. When I open my eyes, a slight blue glow is emanating from it. 

"What?! What is this?! What are you doing?!" Kenway asks, frantically. His frosty breath can't hide his panic. 

*"Eve! No! Wait!"*

I mutter under my frosty breath, "Dilate."

Then the world really goes still, like it has lost its momentum to time. The falling ice particles are not falling anymore. Kenway stops mid-air as he tries to jump behind the desk. 

I turn my body to face the hallway. But just that motion creates a trail of shockwave following every tip of my body. Each shockwave gives my clothes a new tear. Shoulders, chest, stomach, back. I don't care, moving three times faster than sound is the only way I can stop this neutron bomb from leaving this place. 

My right leg contracts, then with all my power kicks the ground, or more precisely, disintegrating it. My body propels forward with unimaginable force, leaving a trail of also frozen in time sonic booms and destruction in its path. In an instant I arrived at the elevator and quickly change my trajectory downwards, breaking through the elevator floor. 

I'm falling down in a fraction of a second before landing with a loud boom shattering the ground on the bottom most floor. A white missile is spewing a frozen-in-time flame in front of me. *Found the payload.* I leap again right to its side panel, tearing through the cover to reveal the wiring and the cluster of smaller missiles inside. I immediately recognize that the bomb inside it has already been armed and there's no way to disarm it. The only way to stop it is to detonate it right now. Blowing everything away, along with me. 

I turn off the dilation mode and the world snaps back to its original pace at once. The rumbles come back stronger, vibration, sound, everything. My clothes mostly torn apart, what's left just clings loosely to my body. Burning pain caused by the air friction from the last few seconds hit me like somebody is peeling off my skin. I should've just listened to AEGIS and took my armor suit. Well, what's done is done, I can't stop now, I need to do one last thing. 

"AEGIS. I can't disarm the missile."

*"Eve! What are you doing?! You're using too much energy too fast!"*

"I know, there's no other choice. I need to detonate it now, before it departs. The thick layer of ice will absorb most of the neutron radiation."

*"But we don't know if you'll survive! Just wait for a second while I run the calculation! I'm running it now!"*

"Time is the one thing we don't have right now, AEGIS. Just keep everything safe until I come back, alright?"

*"No! Wa-"*

"Dilate."

The world stands still once again. I don't need AEGIS's nagging. I know this is what I need to do, and I don't have any regret over it. Besides... This isn't a bad way to go.

I clench my fist and pull it, aiming for the core. In one smooth movement I will break the casing, detonate the core. 

But the universe seems to want to do something else.

A searing pain hit my chest, right at my own core. A pain so painful I can't compare it to when I injected the serum into my spinal cord. It feels like somebody tore apart by chest cavity and punching it non-stop. Dilation mode stops itself, the time runs back again. The pain remains. I can't stop now, not because of this. 

I pull my hand again, and with all the strength I can muster right now, punch the core. Sudden white light floods my vision and everything around me. Then black.
