# Prologue 2 - 

---

*"Eve! Wake up! Eve!"* 

*Warning! Substantial core damage detected!*

*Warning! Substantial core damage detected!*

*Warning! Substantial core damage detected!*

That distant sound of AEGIS's voice doesn't really help me to get my bearing at all, nor the notifications. My head's spinning, my chest feels like it's been blasted by hellfire, wherever hell is. It burns so bad, I've never feel this kind of pain before. I try to roll over so I can push myself up. But the shaking in my arms doesn't help either. 

*"Eve! You have to wake up! There's something really weird going on!"*

*Warning! Coolant supply is running low. Beginning harvest mode.*

*Warning! Low output on harvest mode.*

*Warning! Substantial physical damage detected! Beginning regeneration!*

"Yeah?" I force a coarse voice out of my mouth. "I detected that too. I think there's something wrong with my core."

*"No, Eve. I detect a massive spatial warp near you. What did you do to the neutron bomb?"*

"What?" I manage to stand up and register my surrounding. "A warp?"

Looking left and right, I can't see anything different other than dark ice walls. Looks like that neutron blast obliterated the compound, nothing's left at all. I can safely assume that I'm trapped in this big ice cavity. 

"I'm trapped, AEGIS. Can you see me?"

*"I can't. But I can see something on the ground. Far above you."*

"What's that?"

*"A tear in space."*

I look up, squinting my eyes. I can see light about fifty meters above. Good, I'm not too far under. But when I zoom in, I understand what AEGIS said as a tear in space. 

"AEGIS. Deploy A1."

*"Right away. ETA twenty minutes."*

*Tissue regeneration is successful. Damage repaired.*

I look down to see my torn clothes. I rip my shirt and my pants away before kicking off my boots, leaving me with just my undergarments. Then I kneel down and close my eyes.

"Overclock harvest mode."

*Harvest mode overclocking. Please stay still.* A hissing sound can be heard along with water droplets visibly forming on my exposed skin. 

*Coolant reserve at 15%.*

What I saw above just now is the most bizarre thing I've ever seen. Literally a glowing white tear is forming slowly in the air, like a zipper is being opened. Theoretically that is possible, but something has to trigger it. A tear in the space won't just form willy-nilly. And I don't have a very good feeling about what will come out of it. 

*Coolant reserve at 45%*

"AEGIS. Have you run diagnostic on that thing?"

*"It's currently running. Though I can't say much other than it has a very massive energy output."*

"Like how massive?"

*"Twice that neutron bomb you detonated."*

*Coolant reserve at 60%*

"Hmm. Something must be powering it."

*"Eve, it's getting wider and wider."*

She's right, it's considerably bigger now. Then all of a sudden, a heavy, ground-shattering roar can be heard from it. The ice walls around me shakes heavily. Some of the stalactites are falling down, piercing the ground. What in hell's name is that? Don't tell me a mythical monster is going to come out of it! This isn't some fantasy movie!

*Coolant reserve at 75%*

"Continue your diagnostic. Run the model of Einstein-Rosen bridge. Report back to me once you have it."

*Coolant reseve at 85%*

*"What are you planning to do?"*

*Coolant reserve at 95%*

"Getting out of here. I have to face anything that sent us that roar just now. As far as we know, it can be an apocalyptic monster or just an overgrown polar bear. Either way, we need to know what's happening."

*"But A1 is still eighty minutes out! You're naked Eve!"*

"Yet that thing is coming out NOW."

*Coolant reserve at 100%. Stopped recharging.*

With just a flex, I kick the ground as hard as I can. In an instant, the ground breaks, I move right to the ceiling and break a thin layer of ice. For a brief time, before gravity do its usual thing of pulling me down, I'm letting my momentum keeping me airborne. I can see it clearly now. I'm about ten meters above the ground, yet that... 'gate' is standing twice as tall. Then I see a ripple right in front of me, like if you disturb a water surface. I can feel my heart skips a beat as something really comes out of the ripple. Like a projectile.

"F-"

I jerk my body and pull myself backward, barely avoiding its horn that's grazing my breast, tearing the fabric. I let my body fall down following the gravity as that thing also falls down straight to the ground, sending a loud bang. 

"Why do I keep getting my clothes torn today?" Grumbling to my own. 

*"Told you so."*

That thing rises again from the ground, unscathed. Now I can see its form, and if it didn't just try to stab me in the heart, I'd be mesmerized. A majestic, bipedal creature that has an uncannily similar form to human. The muscles, proportions. If not for that almost all white fur along its body and two forward-pointing horns on its head, I'd say that its a human.
