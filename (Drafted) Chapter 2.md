# Chapter 2 - White Tundra

---

"I need a full debriefing. Now." 

*"Unfortunately, I can't tell you much, Eve. Only that right after you detonated the neutron bomb cluster five years ago, the world changed overnight. These 'gates' showed up first, a lot of it, across the globe. Those gates are the literal origin of the monsters you just faced, among other more bizarre things."*

"Among other? What else are spewing out of it?"

*"Well, from what we know, it's a literal door for creatures to come in from another planet. And some of them are sentient, Eve."*

"My god..."

*"And that's not all. You've heard of enhanced, yes? They are humans, gained powers overnight."*

"It doesn't just end, does it? What kinds of powers?"

*"Super ones. Some are similar to yours, some are completely different. That's why they're called 'enhanced'. It's recorded that about a million living humans are enhanced."*

"And you can't conclude the cause of all this? Because I'm willing to bet that the fact that it happened right after I blacked out wasn't a coincidence."

*"I personally think it's definitely connected to you. But I can't pinpoint why. It's like you caused and not caused these at the same time. I don't have any model capable of analyzing it."*

Then I remember, the searing pain in my chest, right at the core. The pain of getting killed over and over again. The pain I never experienced and will never want to experience again. And now I can't even use dilation mode. These all must be connected somehow. I need to know how.

"Alright. First things first. I need to get out of here. I need to go home."

*"Uhm, about that..."*

"What about it? I can't go back to my own home?"

*"Yeah... You see, because the world went to chaos right after you were missing. The US found the perfect opportunity to strong-arm Kusanagi to submission. They kinda wiped out your board and ransacked the HQ."*

"I can't catch a break at all, can't I?"

*"Because they finally have definitive proof of what Kusanagi is capable of, they did a hostile take over to us."*

"Alright, alright. The fan has spread all the shit that was thrown to it. What's the damage?"

*"As an exception for I.O., Stream as a whole is now owned by the US government. You yourself have been declared dead, but if you really resurface without a proper plan. They'll come for you too."*

"Do they have any idea you exist at all?"

*"They figured that something must be running all these because they judged the board members had too little knowledge. But they haven't found me yet because I made myself invisible through our sub-net. That being said, I can't help you much because it will reveal myself to them along with you."*

"So let me summarize. I lost Stream and everything in it, can't go back to my home, and potentially will lose you too if I move too much?"

*"Yeah, that's about it."*

"And I lost my power too."

*"Wait, what?"*

"I couldn't get into dilation mode at all earlier. Now I have three gashes on my chest because of it."

*"It doesn't make any sense. I ran the numbers multiple times. The blast you detonate might kill you, yes. But it wouldn't just erase your power. Hold on, let me run a quick diagnostic."*

A bunch of fast moving graphics suddenly fills my eyes. "Find anything interesting?"

*"Just that most of your regulatory modules are fried, and I detect a crack on your core. That's why you can't use your power. You need the medical bay at home."*

"Yeah, I figure. Say, can you send my jet here?"

*"Nope, the FBI has its men in the house as we speak. They just can't risk blowing it by triggering the security measures."*

"Right, that makes sense. I don't want them blowing my house either. I'm going to have to use the other safe house. Hold on a sec."

I turn over and approach Kurt who's still sitting mindlessly.

"Kurt, can I ask you a question?"

"Sure, ask away."

"How did you get here?"

"By portal of course. It's not like there's a bus station here, right?"

"Portal?"

"Ah, right. Our ice cavewoman. Portal is the transportation mode people use nowadays. It allows us to move from one place to another instantly."

"And your home is where?"

"Surrey, Canada."

"Canada? Portal can move you that far?"

"Sure it is. Isn't magic awesome?"

"Right, magic. So, can you take me through the portal?"

"Of course. I was going to offer to take you out of here anyway. But where's your home?"

"Surrey is fine. I have some... relatives there."

*"Surrey safe house is... Well, safe. You can use that."* AEGIS informs me unprompted.

---

"I think I've seen you somewhere." Says Kurt breaking the silence. We've been walking for almost an hour inside this ice storm to the portal he said earlier. 

"What makes you think that?" I answer half-heartedly. It will be bad if Kurt recognizes me. I'll lose my only way out of here. But I was last seen five years ago, and humans tend to forget a lot of this. Besides, I don't really look like a proper human at all. Messy wet hair, dark eyes, beaten face, wounded chest. I look closer to a zombie than a human.

"Yeah. But I can't really remember. Anyway, you want to tell me how you ended up in this frozen tundra now? Y'know because we've just had our life-or-death moment together."

"Firstly, you were having your life-or-death moment. I wasn't, I wouldn't be dead because of something like that. And secondly, no, I can't tell you why I'm here at all. It's for your own safety."

The storm subsides. Finally. I can see a glowing white circle in the distance. Is that the portal? It really looks like a space distortion even though the surface is smooth. 

"You don't have to worry about my safety, Eve. You should worry about your own."

"What's that supposed to mea-"

Just by sheer instinct, I move my head backward just by a hundredth of a second before a projectile flies by me at bullet-speed. Missed me by a hair's width. I quickly activate thermal vision and scan the approximate area the bullet was coming from. There are two men standing by with a high-powered sniper rifle. About five hundred meters away.

"Aw, come on!" I curse from the depth of my being. It's really just one after another. "I thought we were friends, Kurt!"

He's now standing quite a bit far from me, maybe just in case his friends miss again.

"The thing is, Eve. A girl like you won't be safe wandering around in your underwear. And the fact that you're the missing CEO of Stream Corp doesn't help at all! We will get a fortune from your bounty!"

"So that's how it is. Fine. You guys asked for it."

I leap to Kurt's direction right before another shot comes. In a flash I'm already at his back, effortlessly grabbing both his arms and cleanly breaking his elbows. A wailing filled with agonizing pain escapes from his mouth. Then I grab his neck and walk forward, using his body as a shield.

"Come out both of you! Or the next thing that will break is his neck!"

VOOM! A glowing red laser beam penetratess instantly through both our left torsos, leaving a gaping hole about the size of a golf ball. Fuck! Really? They are willing to kill their own man?

Kurt vomits out blood from his mouth and nose, immediately collapses and convulses half-dead. Hah, serves you right! I push the ground to the side, quickly moving out of their line of sight and hiding behind a big boulder. 

*Alert! Collapsed lung imminent!*

*Alert! External injury detected! Beginning regeneration on left torso.*

*"Eve, you are injured badly."*

"Yeah, I know, I know. Just need to put my thumb in it. I need you to do some calculations, how many blinks can I chain in my condition now?"

*"Beginning calculations... Two. You can only do two in an hour. Any more will further damage your circuits."*

"Alright, noted. I need just one. Did you see the laser beam earlier?"

*"Yes. It's ours. Kusanagi model SPP14. It's the older model, it needs to recharge for ten minutes between shots."*

"Exactly, now's my chance to counter. I hope they don't have more toys."

*Regeneration completed!*

As if prompted by the notification, I leap over the boulder and start sprinting. A shot comes through, then another, and another, dodged by a hair's width each time. I run faster and faster with each second, occasionally leaping from side to side to confuse the enemies. Little by little, I can make out their shapes. Two men, dressed completely white hiding behind a cluster of rocks. 

Fifty meters left. Dodge. Forty. Thirty. Dodge again. Twenty. Ten. Dodge. Five. *Blink.*

I vanish into thin air. Both men seem surprised and start to scan the surrounding with their scope. While I've already reappeared quietly right behind them. I simply stomp both their legs to incapacitate them. Only then they notice me, or more like wailing painfully at me. I'm going to go deaf from all these wailing. I take both their weapons while they're busy crying over their smashed legs. 

"Look. Guys. Apparently, I've been in this frozen tundra for over five years. Can't say I'm a big fan of the weather here either. So help a girl out, won't you?" I forcefully take one of their furry white coats and the pair of boots they're wearing. "Now that's more like it."

One of them apparently still has a fighting spirit. He takes out his handgun and attempts to fire it at me. That is before I grab the gun and redirect it to the other guy's head, killing him instantly.

"That's not too nice, don't you think?" I throw away the gun and grab his neck. "Now I need you to tell me how to use the portal and what's on the other side. Please." He tries to talk but is impeded by my choking. "Right, sorry about that." I release his neck for him to immediately gasp for air. "Now talk."

"A-alright, okay. Just don't kill me, okay?" He says, grunting and gasping from the pain in his legs. I nod. "To use the portal, you just have to step inside it, then you will emerge on the other side."

"What's on the other side?"

"Nothing. Just some military personnel and immigration officers. They will question you, but it doesn't really matter. They will assume you're an enhanced and most likely won't want to mess with you."

"Thank you, that wasn't too hard was it?"

Then I walk away from him.

"H-Hey! You have to take me back! Please! I can't walk!"

"You asked me to let you live, and I kept my promise. We've never discussed getting you out of here." 

With that, I leap away and start sprinting to the portal.
